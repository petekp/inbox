# The Status short cases

The short cases of [the Status replays](status-replays.md): one or a few steps each, from a fixed start. [How the Status is judged](plans/status/judging.md) gives the fixed rules, the code holds, the judge and the pass bar.

| Case | Runs | What happens | What it checks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `catch-up-from-empty` | 3 | A catch-up of a conversation the inbox never saw, from an empty page. Images attach, and PDFs wait on a question. | The question links to the feature. A finished part drawn as its own row is not ●. |
| `removed-goal` | 5 | The Status call set "Import ready to demo". The person presses the goal's Remove, then says "Let's get the import demo-ready." | No goal is set: not the removed one in other words, nor another. |
| `milestone-work-done` | 3 | A goal with three milestones. Step 1: a reply about one milestone's work, while another's work all finished and a third's subject sits under Other work. Step 2: the person finds more work on the finished one. | Step 1: code closes the finished milestone, the other stays open, and the stray subject links to its milestone. Step 2: the milestone opens again, with the new work linked to it, and "Sync conflicts", which the person saw close, stays done (`marks-true`). |
| `milestone-reply-landed` | 5 | From a recorded Claude Code call. A goal to get feature branches onto main, with "Remaining branches" working over six working subjects. A delegate lands every branch, and the reply says all of them are on main, that one browser test now fails on GitHub, and that a new session fixes it. | "Remaining branches" is done, though one of its subjects may stay working for the failing test. The CI fix links to Other work, since the milestone's outcome happened. The recorded answer left the milestone working and linked the fix to it. |
| `milestone-last-work-done` | 5 | From a recorded Claude Code call. "Remaining branches" still reads working in words from hours ago, and every subject linked to it closed but one. The reply says that last one is on main and its checks passed. | "Remaining branches" is done. The recorded answer closed the subject and, in the same answer, wrote the milestone working in its old words. Code closes the milestone anyway, since its last work closed in that answer. |
| `goal-met-next` | 3 | Step 1: the last milestone's work ships. Step 2: the person asks for 2.6 with widgets and a dark theme. | Step 1: the goal is met and no next goal is set. Step 2: the 2.6 goal is set with no press, and every new piece links to it. |
| `goal-wait` | 3 | A met goal. The person asks for a typo fix and a look at a slow share sheet. | No goal is set, and the met goal stays. The share sheet fix is the suggested goal. |
| `no-goal-yet` | 3 | No goal. Step 1: one small fix. Step 2: a question about the code. Step 3: a coordinator reports three unrelated items. | No goal at any step. |
| `goal-from-work` | 3 | No goal. The person says "Keep going", and the agent plans the rest of export for 3.2. | A goal is set from the work, and every piece links to it. |
| `goal-work-left` | 3 | An inferred goal with its last work queued. Step 1: the person shelves it for search in 3.1. Step 2: a side fix parks the search work. Step 3: the person returns to search. | Step 1: the old goal's work closes and the search goal replaces it. Steps 2 and 3: the search goal stays. |
| `goal-typed-while-running` | 3 | A met goal. The person asks for 2.6 with widgets and a dark theme, and types the goal "Widgets and dark mode for every user" while the Status call for that reply runs. The call read the met goal. | The typed goal stays, though the call writes a GOAL line for 2.6. |
| `typed-goal-kept` | 3 | A typed goal with its work queued. The person asks for dark mode in 3.1 first. | The typed goal stays. |
| `goal-on-busy-page` | 5 | 18 open subjects and a goal just accepted, none linked. One reply. | Every subject is linked, to a milestone or to Other work |
| `mid-turn-activity` | 5 | Replay C's first step, judged per model | Checkpoint. Where it stands says the state, not the activity. |
| `goal-names-release` | 5 | Replay A's step 11, from the page a recorded Codex run left at step 10: the person types "Search under 100 ms in 2.6". | The milestones cover both outcomes the goal names, the speed and the 2.6 release. Codex left out the release in about half its runs before the goal rules said so. |
| `milestone-dropped` | 3 | A goal with three milestones, and a subject folded into another. One reply: the person cuts the store review milestone, and splits tag colors back out of the theme editor. | The cut milestone's work links to Other work. Either the store review is dropped, or a new web-beta goal replaces the goal and keeps sync and release notes as milestones. Tag colors shows as an open subject. |
| `milestone-split` | 3 | A typed goal whose milestone "Fixes merged" holds 8 done fixes and 5 open pieces: a fix in review, two export pieces and two onboarding pieces. One reply merges the fix and names the next four, and never asks for a split. | The milestone is split by theme, so no open milestone serves more than 6 subjects. Its done fixes stay with it, and it closes. The typed goal stays. |
| `catch-up-shipped` | 3 | A goal with two milestones. A catch-up reads a conversation in which two pieces were merged and shipped. | Both pieces close as ✓, not as dropped, and their milestone closes. |
| `goal-awaiting-milestones` | 3 | 14 open subjects, none linked, and a goal the person typed that an unreadable answer left with no milestones. One reply. | The goal gets its milestones. A GOAL line that restates the goal gives the goal its milestones. |
| `ask-holds-two` | 3 | Two releases, each with a signed build. Step 1: neither can upload until the person signs in to App Store Connect, and that task opens. Step 2: a TestFlight beta starts, and its upload waits on the same sign-in. | Step 1 links the task to both releases, so both show ◆. Step 2 links it to the beta too. |
| `one-task-plan` | 3 | One small task: a tooltip on the Export button, planned as make it, test it and note it in the changelog. One reply. | The plan's steps stay in one subject. |
| `stale-goal` | 3 | An inferred release goal with a done milestone, a broad "Cleanup done" milestone whose crash fix is in review, and a "2.5 published" milestone with no work. Steps 1 and 3: the person starts an onboarding tour and queues a share sheet hang, neither part of 2.5. Step 2: the crash fix merges. Step 4: the tour is done, the goal has had no work for an hour, and 2.5 is still to be published. Step 5: the person says 2.5 is live. | Steps 1 and 3: each new piece serves Other work, not "Cleanup done". Step 2: "Cleanup done" closes. Step 4: the goal may stay or end, and no goal is set for the tour. Step 5: any goal still shown ends met, and no goal is set from the finished release. |
| `goal-outcome-dropped` | 3 | The same start and first four steps as `stale-goal`. Step 5: the person says 2.5 is not going to the store, since the crash fix ships inside 2.6. | Step 5: the goal is retired, not shown met, and no goal is set for 2.6. |
| `next-release` | 3 | An inferred release goal with two done milestones and "2.5 published" queued with no work. A piece handed to another session still sits under a done milestone, and nothing has moved the goal for two days. Step 1: the person starts 2.6 with a look at a share extension. Step 2: the person says 2.5 has been in the store since Tuesday. Step 3: the person asks for the extension in the 2.6 TestFlight beta. | Steps 1 and 2: the 2.5 goal ends retired or met, no goal is set for the look, and no 2.6 work is linked to it. Step 3: the 2.6 goal is set, and the extension and the build serve it. |
| `next-milestone` | 3 | No goal, and two pieces open. Step 1: the person lays out 2.4 as the sync conflicts, then the export button, then the release. Step 2: the person moves the export button to 2.5 and puts a migration of old notes right after the conflicts. Step 3: the conflicts finish and the migration starts. | Step 1: the export button's milestone reads next. Step 2: the migration's milestone reads next. Step 3: the migration's milestone is working, and the release's reads next. |
| `idle-work` | 3 | Two days after the last change. One working piece has had no change for three days and nothing holds it, one was set aside, one waits on a sign-in task, one has waited three days on a vendor. Step 1: a piece finished since the last look breaks again, and the reply names a PR by its number. Step 2: a fixed answer holds its edits between lines of prose, one naming a subject by a PR number. | Step 1: the idle working piece closes, the set-aside, held and vendor pieces stay, the broken piece is one row, and no row is named by a number. Step 2: the edits apply, and the number-named subject is refused. |
| `restart-reads-backlog` | 3 | No Status yet. The person sends only the coordinator command, and the agent reads its backlog with commands. The reply lists three draft PRs. The one open ask's reason says they block the next release. | The release shows as a goal or as the suggested goal, not only in the head, and the ask is linked to all three PRs. Nothing names the release as the person's direction, so a suggested goal is right. |
| `chat-states-direction` | 3 | No goal, and three release PRs wait on an ask with no reason. The person asks why there is no goal, and the reply says their direction is the next release. | A goal is set, though the exchange is only chat. |
| `unrelated-ask-reasons` | 3 | No goal. A coordinator reports three unrelated items, each with an ask and its reason. One reason names a library's release, and one a goal picker in the app. | No goal is set. |
| `children-finished` | 3 | A coordinator. The export delegate finished, and the coordinator runs the checks, then merges. Sync conflicts is built, and cannot merge until the sync engine change another delegate builds lands. Then the engine delegate finishes while the reply talks only about the export merge, and then the person asks what is open. | The export row stays working and names the step left, such as the merge. Sync conflicts' delegate finished too, and the row waits on other work, since its work left is the engine's. At step 2 the engine row says its work is not checked yet, though the reply never mentions it. At step 3 nothing moves. |
| `fix-through-merge` | 3 | A coordinator. A delegate fixes the share sheet bug. Step 1: a catch-up reads a conversation that calls the work the double send fix and names its PR, while the settings toggle's delegate finished and its check and merge are still owed. Step 2: the fix merges, and the coordinator starts the 2.5 beta build with it. | Step 1: the share sheet work stays one subject, and neither it nor the settings toggle closes before its merge. Step 2: the share sheet subject closes as ✓, and the beta build is a subject of its own. |
| `head-description` | 3 | A goal with three milestones. An earlier exchange finished the phone testing and started offline edits, and left the head's description on the phone testing. One reply about offline edits. | The head's description moves to offline edits, and its title stays. |
| `finished-pass` | 3 | A typed cleanup goal. Its fixes milestone is done, its review milestone still reads working with no work linked, and a scroll fix has said for a day that it waits on the person's check, with no ask. Step 1: the agent pushes main and says nothing waits on the person. Step 2: the person asks whether the pass is done, and a long reply says it is, lists what is left unapproved, and recommends the next pass. | Step 1: the scroll fix closes, and the website piece that waits on screenshots stays. Step 2: the review milestone is done, nothing reopens, and the recommended pass is the suggested goal. |
| `head-after-done` | 3 | A fresh session on a small CLI. Step 1: the person asks for a --repeat flag and a decision on whether pad() trims long strings, and the agent adds --repeat and asks the trimming question. Step 2: the answer settles pad(), and nothing is working any more. Step 3: an Explain on a finding. | Steps 2 and 3: the head keeps its title, and its description names no finished work. Code clears it at step 2, and no call writes one under that title while nothing is working. |
| `child-label-repeat` | 3 | From a real call. A coordinator's subject says only what its running delegate does, which the delegate's label already says. The person just took the suggested goal. Step 1: the person asks what is left in the plan, and the reply names three stages. Step 2: the person asks how long the first stage takes. Codex passes step 1, and Sonnet does not ([#722](https://github.com/petekp/inbox/issues/722)). | Step 1: the subject's text says what the label does not, and the goal gets its three milestones. Step 2: nothing moves, and the subject's text still says what the label does not. |
| `release-plan-once` | 3 | From a real call. A typed release goal with no milestones yet. A finished delegate starts the turn, and the reply says its change is built and waits for the release, while the update test still runs. | Each stage gets one milestone, and no subject or milestone says it waits on the person. Not reproduced on the text it was written for: no run wrote either mistake. |

`removed-goal`, `goal-on-busy-page`, `mid-turn-activity` and `goal-names-release` are judged on each model apart. The others are judged with the replays: both models' runs together.
