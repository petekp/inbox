| A row's title and status: a collapsed title wraps to two lines, then is cut with an ellipsis at the end of its second line, after the last whole word that fits, or inside a word only when one word is wider than a line. An open row shows its whole title. A status, on an open row or a collapsed one, follows the title when the title and the whole status fit on one line, and otherwise takes lines of its own under it, whole. A ` · ` separator never starts or ends a line. A title's last punctuation stays on the line with its word. Codex and Herdr draw parts with a hidden dot slot (`codex/src/tab-parted.tsx`, `herdr/src/paint-parted.ts`). Herdr does the same with hidden copies it measures (`herdr/src/title-cut.ts:cutTitleOf`), and clips the whole title at two lines until they are measured. Herdr's text layout breaks a line before punctuation that does not fit, so Herdr also measures a copy without the title's last punctuation. When the whole title is taller, it draws the title with a line break before its last word, or in a title with no space before the character its punctuation follows (`herdr/src/title-cut.ts:wrappedTitleOf`). A settled title's one-line cut comes from the same copies in Herdr (`herdr/src/title-cut.ts:oneLineTitleOf`), from a hidden one-line copy at its line's width in Codex (`codex/src/tab-title-cut.tsx:SettledTitlePart`), from `settledTitleLine` on desktop, from `terminalSettledTitle` in the terminal, and from `omp/src/panel-text.ts:cutSettledTitle` in omp. | A row's result mark: a closed or settled row's, a handed-off row's, and an open row's while the agent works on its Talk press. Its refused-notification Undo, and a settled label's tone | `core/view/help.ts:CLOSED_MARKS`, `core/view/rows.ts:closedMark`, `core/view/rows.ts:settledMark`, `core/view/rows.ts:rowResultMark`, `core/view/rows.ts:ResultMark`, `core/view/rows.ts:canUndoRefused`, `core/view/rows.ts:settledTone`, `core/view/rows.ts:closedTitleTone`, `core/view/arrivals.ts:closedTitleClock`, `core/view/marks.ts:mutedMarkShape` | All four looks. A closed row draws ✓ when handled, ✕ when the person dismissed it, and ✗ when it lapsed: the agent or the inbox model closed it because the work overtook it, or the list was full. Desktop and Codex draw ✗ as a ballot X shape, and the terminal and omp draw ⊘ in its place, so it reads apart from ✕ (see "The cancelled and waiting marks" in Differences on purpose). A settled row draws the mark its Closed line will draw. A handed-off row draws ✓ in the done tone once its send arrives or its open or copy works. A reply sent after the item closed settles with its Closed line's mark too. While what a row sent is sending, its mark runs, as on the Status. While a closed row's message waits for the agent's turn to end, its mark is the queued ○, muted and still, so the row does not read as active while its label says "Queued". An open or handed-off row's mark runs while its message waits, since the running mark says the message is on its way and the row is still open. While it is held for Undo, unconfirmed or refused, the mark is blank and keeps its place. An open row's mark runs while its Talk press reads "Explaining…" or another working label, so a long reply never looks like a stuck press. The terminal and omp draw it before the label, desktop and Codex in the mark column. Where each look draws it: see "Result marks" in Differences on purpose. The terminal and omp draw a settled label in the done tone only once nothing it sent is on its way. Desktop and Codex draw it as muted text after the title. A settled row's title takes a tint from how the row closed, from the press on, and holds then fades on the clock of a new row's tint, from when a look first draws the row closed (`core/view/arrivals.ts:closedTitleClock`), so it goes even while the row stays in place. A settled PR takes none. |
| Held rows: which closed rows stay in place while the person replies, what their reply sends, and which closed records a prune keeps | `core/view/timing.ts:heldReplies`, `core/view/view.ts:inboxView`, `core/view/rows.ts:fieldExit`, `core/view/timing.ts:keptDiscards`, `core/view/press-reducer.ts:applyPress`, `core/runtime/session-presses.ts:pruned` | All four looks. The Codex tab sends its replies and discarded replies with each view and press. Every write in the mod, Codex and omp runs `core/runtime/session-presses.ts:pruned`.
