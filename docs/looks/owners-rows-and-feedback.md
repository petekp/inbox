# Owners: rows, presses and feedback

Part of [the look map](../looks.md#who-owns-each-rule).

| Rule | Owner | Read by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Row state | `hooks/view.ts:RowState`, `hooks/presses.ts:isHandedOff` | All four looks |
| A row's result mark: a closed or settled row's, and a handed-off row's. Its refused-notification Undo, and a settled label's tone | `hooks/view.ts:CLOSED_MARKS`, `hooks/view.ts:closedMark`, `hooks/view.ts:settledMark`, `hooks/view.ts:rowResultMark`, `hooks/view.ts:ResultMark`, `hooks/view.ts:canUndoRefused`, `hooks/view.ts:settledTone`, `hooks/marks.ts:cancelledMarkShape` | All four looks. A closed row draws ✓ when handled, ✕ when the person dismissed it, and ✗ when it lapsed: the agent or the inbox model closed it because the work overtook it, or the list was full. ✗ is the ballot X, with a long upstroke, so it reads apart from ✕. Desktop and Codex draw it as a shape (see "The cancelled mark" in Differences on purpose). A settled row draws the mark its Closed line will draw. A handed-off row draws ✓ in the done tone once its send arrives or its open or copy works, and its label stays muted. A reply sent after the item closed settles with its Closed line's mark too. While what a row sent is on its way, its mark runs, as on the Status. While it is unconfirmed or refused, the mark is blank and keeps its place. Where each look draws it: see "Result marks" in Differences on purpose. The terminal and omp draw a settled label in the done tone only once nothing it sent is on its way. Desktop and Codex draw it as muted text after the title. |
| Held rows: which closed rows stay in place while the person replies, what their reply sends, and which closed records a prune keeps | `hooks/view.ts:heldReplies`, `hooks/view.ts:inboxView`, `hooks/view.ts:fieldExit`, `hooks/view.ts:keptDiscards`, `hooks/presses.ts:applyPress`, `hooks/session.ts:pruned` | All four looks. The mod passes its open field, drafts and discarded replies. The omp panel keeps its field and drafts in its ui state and passes them with each screen and press. The Codex tab sends its replies and discarded replies with each view and press. Every write in the mod, Codex and omp runs `hooks/session.ts:pruned`. It keeps the closed records of the rows `heldRows` names, and of the rows closed or pressed since `shownSince`. So a row drawn in place keeps its record through more closes than `hooks/ledger.ts:MAX_CLOSED`. The mod's `hooks/register.tsx:changeSession` sets both fields at each write, from its drafts and its showing. A write to a conversation the pane does not draw clears both, since that conversation holds no rows and has no showing. The Codex server sets both at each view and press. Each view replaces the last, since Codex shows one tab per thread, as `tabSeenAt` also assumes. Codex is not told when a tab closes, so its showing lasts until the next view, and every close in between keeps its record. omp sets both from its panel ui at each press and when the panel shows another tab. It sets `shownSince` alone when the panel opens or closes. A press, a Codex view and an omp tab change set both through `hooks/session.ts:withHolds`. |
| Feedback text | `hooks/view.ts:feedbackText`, `hooks/view.ts:feedbackOf`, `hooks/view.ts:markedFeedbackText` | Terminal and desktop through the pane, Codex through the tab, omp through the panel |
| Where a press status sits on an open row: under the buttons while its press is out, including an open or a copy, then after the title. A row that still waits after its press, such as a failing check after Fix, keeps its done status under the buttons | `hooks/view.ts:isStatusUnderActions`, `hooks/view.ts:isPressOut`, `hooks/view.ts:isAfterTitle`, `hooks/view.ts:STILL_WAITING_AFTER` | All four looks. Only the terminal and desktop looks read `isAfterTitle`, which puts a failure on its own line. |
| Which button's press is out: it draws dim and ignores another press, by click or key, until the press settles. A note on its row, such as "This changed before your press", does not settle it. A press that reads Not confirmed and a reply are never out. | `hooks/view.ts:outAction` over `hooks/view.ts:pressFeedbackIn`, the row's feedback without its note, read into `ActionView.isOut` and `PrAction.isOut`; `hooks/session.ts:pressOn` ignores the press by the same rule | All four looks draw it in their own way: see "Pressed button while its press is out" in Differences on purpose. |
| Done labels | `hooks/view.ts:DONE_LABELS` | All four looks |
| Closed-row outcome labels | `hooks/view.ts:outcomeLabel`, set on each closed entry by `hooks/view.ts:inboxView` | All four looks |
| Row content and reply intent | `hooks/view.ts:RowView`, `hooks/view.ts:rowTitle`, `hooks/view.ts:replyHint`, `hooks/view.ts:rowFeedbackText` | All four looks |
| Action order, emphasis and icon kind | `hooks/view.ts:ActionView`, `hooks/view.ts:actionsOf`, `hooks/view.ts:rowKeyPlan` | All four looks |
| Shared labels and fold counts | `hooks/view.ts:ROW_COPY`, `hooks/view.ts:actionLabel`, `hooks/view.ts:foldLabel`, `hooks/view.ts:earlierLabel`, `hooks/view.ts:groupEmptyText`, `hooks/view.ts:DEMO_NOTICE` | All four looks |
| Key plan | `hooks/view.ts:rowKeyPlan` | All four looks take the order. Terminal and omp draw the key letters. Desktop and Codex draw labels only, and Codex still binds the letters. omp calls it with `isAllShown` true. |
| Where a row's follow-ups wrap: on its buttons' last line when all of them fit there, else together from the next line, where they wrap among themselves | `hooks/view.ts:actionLines` | All four looks. The terminal and omp lay out with `actionLines`, in cells. The terminal draws the follow-ups as a second row when it starts them on a new line (`hooks/register.tsx:actionRows`), counts the `·` it draws before follow-ups that share the line, and counts the lines in `keyLines`. omp lays out each button line with it in `omp/src/panel.ts:buttonLines`. Desktop and Codex draw the same rule with flex-wrap, which uses each control's real width. Desktop puts the links Client in one Box as wide as all its links. The row moves that Box whole to the next line when it does not fit, and shrinks it to the line when it is wider (`hooks/register.tsx:followUpLinks`). Codex puts its follow-ups in one `.follow-ups` box that moves the same way and wraps inside itself (`codex/src/row-actions.tsx:RowActions`, `codex/src/tab.html`). |
| Press reducer and effects | `hooks/presses.ts:applyPress`, `hooks/presses.ts:runLocalEffects`, `hooks/presses.ts:runSends` | The mod and omp through `hooks/session-host.ts:startPress`, and the Codex server |
| When a sent reply's draft is deleted | `hooks/presses.ts:isPressConfirmed` | All four looks. A draft is deleted only after a confirmed press. A failed, stale or unconfirmed send keeps it, so Reply opens with the words. The mod's `runPress`, the Codex tab's `sendReply` and omp's `createPanel` each call it. While the send is still out, the looks differ: see "Reply while its send is out" in Differences on purpose. |
| Marks and their legend: one meaning for each glyph wherever a look draws it | `hooks/view.ts:SUBJECT_MARKS`, `hooks/view.ts:HELD_MARK`, `hooks/view.ts:RUNNING_FRAMES`, `hooks/view.ts:CLOSED_MARKS`, `hooks/view.ts:FINDING_GROUPS`, `hooks/view.ts:PR_MARKS` give each mark. `hooks/view.ts:MARK_LEGEND` lists each glyph once, with its words, its tone and whether it runs. A test in `tests/status.test.ts` fails when a mark in those records has no legend entry. | All four looks draw the legend on their Help page with the code that draws their rows' marks: the terminal and omp as text, with the running mark's frames. Desktop draws a toned mark's shape from `hooks/marks.ts:markShape` where it has one, ✗ from `hooks/marks.ts:cancelledMarkShape`, ✕ as text, and the Svg dot for the running mark. Codex draws ✗ as its shape and fades the running mark. |
| Settled rows: a row closed or pressed since the look began showing its tab stays in place with its result. It folds when the person sends a message they typed, switches tab or closes the inbox. A message a press sent does not fold it. A row whose Undo still lasts stays through a typed message and folds when Undo ends. Undo lasts `SETTLED_MS` from the close. A PR dismissed during the showing settles the same way. | `hooks/view.ts:isSettledInPlace`, `hooks/view.ts:isShownSince`, `hooks/view.ts:isDrawnInPlace`, `hooks/view.ts:inboxView`, `hooks/view.ts:closedRowState`, `hooks/pr-tab.ts:drawnPrs`, `hooks/session.ts:messageTyped`, `hooks/view.ts:SETTLED_MS` | All four looks. Each host reports a message the person typed with `messageTyped`, which saves its time as `presence.lastTypedMessageAt`. The mod reports a `prompt.submit` whose origin is `composer` or `bridge` (`hooks/register.tsx:on('prompt.submit')`). Codex reports a main agent's `UserPromptSubmit` whose text matches no press's sent record, since Codex names no source for a prompt (`codex/src/hook.ts:handleHook`). omp reports an `input` event whose source is `interactive` (`omp/src/host.ts`). omp fires that only for what the person submits in its editor, never for `sendUserMessage`. omp's panel takes every key while it shows. So the person types only after the panel closes, and closing it has already folded the rows. Each look passes the time it began showing its tab as `shownSince` in its holds. The mod passes it from `hooks/register.tsx:syncShown`, which runs where the mod writes the shown tab, when the pane opens or closes, when a conversation loads, and for the sample. omp passes it from its panel ui, and the Codex tab with each view and press. A look that draws from a view it read before a switch checks each row and PR with `isDrawnInPlace` again. A drawn PR carries a row's state. `closedRowState` builds a dismissed PR's settled state as it builds a dismissed row's. The mod and omp pass `SETTLED_MS` as `settleWindowMs`, and Codex passes `codex/src/settle.ts:SETTLE_WINDOW_MS`. It sets `canUndo` for rows and PRs alike. On desktop and in the Codex tab, a settled title wraps beside Undo. So a row or PR that offered Undo keeps Undo's space after Undo ends, and its title keeps its wrap. Both read `offersUndo`. The terminal draws Undo on the result's line under the title, and omp cuts a settled row's one line to the panel's width, so neither moves when Undo ends. |
