| A row's result mark: a closed or settled row's, a handed-off row's, and an open row's while the agent works on its Talk press. The mod's `hooks/register.tsx:changeSession` sets both fields at each write, from its drafts and its showing. A write to a conversation the pane does not draw clears both, since that conversation holds no rows and has no showing. The Codex server sets both at each view and press. Each view replaces the last, since Codex shows one tab per thread, as `tabSeenAt` also assumes. Codex is not told when a tab closes, so its showing lasts until the next view, and every close in between keeps its record. omp sets both from its panel ui at each press and when the panel shows another tab. | Settled rows: a row closed or pressed since the look began showing its tab stays in place with its result for `SETTLED_MS`, the time Undo lasts, then folds into its group's Closed fold. It folds sooner when the person switches tab or closes the inbox, or sends a message they typed once its Undo has ended. A message a press sent does not fold it. A row stays past that time while its message is held for Undo or on its way to the agent (`hooks/timing.ts:isSendOnItsWay`), so a redraw at Undo end never folds it before its message leaves, and while a press that began before it came due is still under way on a row of its tab: a talk press waiting on its reply, a message sending, or an open or copy running (`hooks/feedback.ts:pressUnderWaySince`, `hooks/timing.ts:isPressOutSince`). A refused or unconfirmed message does not hold it, and its Closed line shows what happened. It also waits while the person has been using the list since before it came due, until `FOLD_QUIET_MS` after their last press, key, scroll or pointer move. A hold keeps only a row that has not folded yet: a press or input that begins after the row came due leaves it folded, and a message typed later never moves its due time back. Once folded, a row stays folded until the look shows its tab again. A PR dismissed during the showing settles the same way. | `hooks/timing.ts:isSettledInPlace`, `hooks/timing.ts:FOLD_QUIET_MS`, `hooks/timing.ts:withListInput`, `hooks/timing.ts:nextFoldAt`, `hooks/timing.ts:isShownSince`, `hooks/timing.ts:isDrawnInPlace`, `hooks/view.ts:inboxView`, `hooks/rows.ts:closedRowState`, `hooks/pr-views.ts:drawnPrs`, `hooks/session-turns.ts:messageTyped`, `hooks/timing.ts:SETTLED_MS` | All four looks. Each look passes the person's input on the list as `listInput` in its holds: the mod from `hooks/register.tsx:noteListInput`, the Codex tab from `codex/src/list-input.ts`, and omp from its panel's keys. The view gives each tab's press hold as `pressOutSince`, when the oldest press still under way there began. The Codex tab adds the presses it has not heard back on, from `codex/src/tab-state.ts:sendingSince`, and times each row's due from the poll that first listed it (`codex/src/settle.ts:isTabInPlace`). The mod redraws at `nextFoldAt`, and omp's redraw timer runs while `nextFoldAt` names a time. Each host reports a message the person typed with `messageTyped`, which saves its time as `presence.lastTypedMessageAt`. The mod reports a `prompt.submit` whose origin is `composer` or `bridge` (`hooks/register.tsx:on('prompt.submit')`). Codex reports a main agent's `UserPromptSubmit` whose text matches no press's sent record, since Codex names no source for a prompt (`codex/src/hook.ts:handleHook`). omp reports an `input` event whose source is `interactive` (`omp/src/host.ts`). omp fires that only for what the person submits in its editor, never for `sendUserMessage`. omp's panel takes every key while it shows. So the person types only after the panel closes, and closing it has already folded the rows. Each look passes the time it began showing its tab as `shownSince` in its holds. The mod passes it from `hooks/register.tsx:syncShown`, which runs where the mod writes the shown tab, when the pane opens or closes, when a conversation loads, and for the sample. omp passes it from its panel ui, and the Codex tab with each view and press. A look that draws from a view it read before a switch checks each row and PR with `isDrawnInPlace` again. A drawn PR carries a row's state. `closedRowState` builds a dismissed PR's settled state as it builds a dismissed row's. The mod and omp pass `SETTLED_MS` as `settleWindowMs`, and Codex passes `codex/src/settle.ts:SETTLE_WINDOW_MS`. It sets `canUndo` for rows and PRs alike. On desktop and in the Codex tab, a settled title wraps beside Undo. So a row or PR that offered Undo keeps Undo's space after Undo ends, and its title keeps its wrap. Both read `offersUndo`. The terminal draws Undo on the result's line under the title, and omp cuts a settled row's one line to the panel's width, so neither moves when Undo ends. |
