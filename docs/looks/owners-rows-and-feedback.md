| A row's title and status: a collapsed title wraps to two lines, then is cut with an ellipsis at the end of its second line, after the last whole word that fits, or inside a word only when one word is wider than a line. An open row shows its whole title. A status, on an open row or a collapsed one, follows the title when the title and the whole status fit on one line, and otherwise takes lines of its own under it, whole. A ` · ` separator never starts or ends a line. A title's last punctuation stays on the line with its word. Codex and Herdr draw parts with a hidden dot slot (`codex/src/tab-parted.tsx`, `herdr/src/paint-parted.ts`). In Codex a title is a part of its row's or PR heading's `.parted` line, so a status that does not fit beside the whole title moves under it. Codex clamps a closed title to two lines in CSS, and `codex/src/tab-title-cut.tsx:TitlePart` puts the cut at a word: it tries each cut in a hidden copy at the title's width, and again when the width changes. Herdr does the same with hidden copies it measures (`herdr/src/title-cut.ts:cutTitleOf`), and clips the whole title at two lines until they are measured. Herdr's text layout breaks a line before punctuation that does not fit, so Herdr also measures a copy without the title's last punctuation. When the whole title is taller, it draws the title with a line break before its last word, or in a title with no space before the character its punctuation follows (`herdr/src/title-cut.ts:wrappedTitleOf`). A settled title's one-line cut comes from the same copies in Herdr (`herdr/src/title-cut.ts:oneLineTitleOf`), from a hidden one-line copy at its line's width in Codex (`codex/src/tab-title-cut.tsx:SettledTitlePart`), from `settledTitleLine` on desktop, from `terminalSettledTitle` in the terminal, and from `omp/src/panel-text.ts:cutSettledTitle` in omp. The terminal cuts the second line in `hooks/pane/pane-row-lines.tsx`, desktop in `desktopSpanLines`, and omp in `titleLines` |
| A PR hand-off press (Resolve conflicts, Fix, Address, Address all, Merge): its row reads "Resolving conflicts…", "Fixing…", "Addressing…" or "Merging…" with its button out and without "again" until the turn that followed it ends, answered or not. The button then stays out, and ignores presses, until a fetch has read the PR after that turn, or `PR_READ_WAIT_MS` has passed. Every look redraws when the wait ends. It then reads "… again" if the PR still shows the problem. A hand-off pressed while the saved PR predates that turn reads the PR first, and sends nothing once the problem is gone. A hand-off in progress does not hold the PRs tab's other rows in place. | `core/view/feedback.ts:feedbackOf`, `core/view/feedback.ts:prTabHoldSince`, `core/domain/last-actions.ts:repliedAtTurnEnd`, `core/view/pr-press.ts:lastPressOf`, `core/runtime/session-presses.ts:isRepeatWhileOut`, `core/view/pr-press.ts:isViewBeforeReply`, `core/view/timing.ts:timedChangeAt` | Every look, through the shared plans. | Row motion: how a row enters, opens, closes, settles and leaves, and how long each move lasts | `core/view/row-motion.ts:stepRow`, `core/view/row-motion.ts:ROW_STATE_MS` | The Codex tab, and the Herdr panel through `herdr/src/motion.ts`. | A row's status line held while a press puts its status under its buttons: the line it drew stays, and a failure's keeps its space without its words while the press is under way. Once no press is under way, the line stays while the status stays under the buttons and is not a failure's. Otherwise the hold lets go: the line keeps its words while it fades out, then the look closes its space, so no empty space stays | `core/view/held-line.ts:stepHeldLine` | The Codex tab, after the title, through `codex/src/tab-held-after.ts:holdAfter`, and the Herdr panel, above the buttons, through `herdr/src/held-line.ts:holdLinesAbove`. The terminal, desktop and omp hold no line: a press's status there replaces the row's status where it stands. |
| A row's result mark: a closed or settled row's, a handed-off row's, and an open row's while the agent works on its Talk press. Its refused-notification Undo, and a settled label's tone | `core/view/help.ts:CLOSED_MARKS`, `core/view/rows.ts:closedMark`, `core/view/rows.ts:settledMark`, `core/view/rows.ts:rowResultMark`, `core/view/rows.ts:ResultMark`, `core/view/rows.ts:canUndoRefused`, `core/view/rows.ts:settledTone`, `core/view/rows.ts:closedTitleTone`, `core/view/arrivals.ts:closedTitleClock`, `core/view/marks.ts:mutedMarkShape` | All four looks. A closed row draws ✓ when handled, ✕ when the person dismissed it, and ✗ when it lapsed: the agent or the inbox model closed it because the work overtook it, or the list was full. Desktop and Codex draw ✗ as a ballot X shape, and the terminal and omp draw ⊘ in its place, so it reads apart from ✕ (see "The cancelled and waiting marks" in Differences on purpose). A settled row draws the mark its Closed line will draw. A handed-off row draws ✓ in the done tone once its send arrives or its open or copy works. A reply sent after the item closed settles with its Closed line's mark too. While what a row sent is sending, its mark runs, as on the Status. While a closed row's message waits for the agent's turn to end, its mark is the queued ○, muted and still, so the row does not read as active while its label says "Queued". An open or handed-off row's mark runs while its message waits, since the running mark says the message is on its way and the row is still open. While it is held for Undo, unconfirmed or refused, the mark is blank and keeps its place. An open row's mark runs while its Talk press reads "Explaining…" or another working label, so a long reply never looks like a stuck press. The terminal and omp draw it before the label, desktop and Codex in the mark column. Where each look draws it: see "Result marks" in Differences on purpose. The terminal and omp draw a settled label in the done tone only once nothing it sent is on its way. Desktop and Codex draw it as muted text after the title. A settled row's title takes a tint from how the row closed, from the press on, and holds then fades on the clock of a new row's tint, from when a look first draws the row closed (`core/view/arrivals.ts:closedTitleClock`), so it goes even while the row stays in place. A settled PR takes none. |
| Held rows: which closed rows stay in place while the person replies, what their reply sends, and which closed records a prune keeps | `core/view/timing.ts:heldReplies`, `core/view/view.ts:inboxView`, `core/view/rows.ts:fieldExit`, `core/view/timing.ts:keptDiscards`, `core/view/press-reducer.ts:applyPress`, `core/runtime/session-presses.ts:pruned` | All four looks. The mod passes its open field, drafts and discarded replies. The omp panel keeps its field and drafts in its ui state and passes them with each screen and press, and the host keeps drafts for later panels. The Codex tab sends its replies and discarded replies with each view and press. Every write in the mod, Codex and omp runs `core/runtime/session-presses.ts:pruned`. It keeps the closed records of the rows `heldRows` names, and of the rows closed or pressed since `shownSince`. So a row drawn in place keeps its record through more closes than `core/domain/ledger-close.ts:MAX_CLOSED`. The mod's `hooks/mod/mod-session-write.ts:changeSession` sets both fields at each write, from its drafts and its showing. A write to a conversation the pane does not draw clears both, since that conversation holds no rows and has no showing. The Codex server sets both at each view and press. Each view replaces the last, since Codex shows one tab per thread, as `tabSeenAt` also assumes. Codex is not told when a tab closes, so its showing lasts until the next view, and every close in between keeps its record. omp sets both from its panel ui at each press and when the panel shows another tab. It sets `shownSince` alone at open and `heldRows` at close. A press, a Codex view and an omp tab change set both through `core/runtime/session-presses.ts:withHolds`. |
| Drawn text holds no terminal control. That covers text from the agent, the inbox model, GitHub, the agent's own dialog and the banners' host text, such as an API error. The saved session keeps the text as written. In the view, ids, collapsed rows' ids, presses and the drawn versions keep it too, and so do the stored item, finding and answer text a look matches a press with. The host matches each press against the saved ledger. A check's key is built from its drawable name, so a look's key and the host's key agree. | `core/domain/text.ts:drawableText`, `core/domain/text.ts:withDrawableText`, `core/view/session-view.ts:sessionView` with `STORED_KEYS`, `core/view/dialog-row.ts:dialogTitle`, `core/domain/prs.ts:checkKey`, `core/view/banners.ts:bannerOf` and `stopText`, `core/remote/herdr.ts:herdrTokens` | All five looks, through `sessionView`, and the Herdr sidebar line through `herdrTokens`.
