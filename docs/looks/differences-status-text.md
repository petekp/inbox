# Differences on purpose: text levels

Part of [the look map](../looks.md#differences-on-purpose).

| Topic | Terminal | Desktop | Codex | Why | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Faint text (`status-faint-text`) | A done (✓) Status row's name and the line under it, where it stands, its fold's chevron and count, and every Prev line, draw in `pal.faint`, a step below muted: `FAINT_TEXT`, muted faded toward the theme's background until it reads at `FAINT_TEXT_CONTRAST`, 3:1. On a selected row they draw in `pal.selectionFaint`, muted faded toward the selection color until it reads at 3:1 there. The light-daltonized palette fades its own muted. The ANSI themes, and a theme the mod does not know, draw it in `inactive`, as muted. A done milestone folded to its name keeps the fold button's dim attribute, which terminals draw near the faint level. | One translucent color, `rgba(123, 121, 116, 0.8)`, at 3:1 on white and on the dark page, `#191919`. A done milestone folded to its name keeps the fold's color, `#85837e`, a step above faint. | `--faint`: the host's muted mixed into the page, 51% in dark and 95% in light, 3:1 on Codex's colors in each. A page with no host colors falls back to faint at 6.2:1 in light and 2.9:1 in dark. | A theme key cannot be faded, and the desktop app resolves a color the same in both its themes, so one translucent color serves both pages. Codex's light muted text already reads at 3.2:1, so faint fades it only a little there. | `core/view/tones.ts:faintOver`, `core/view/status-page-parts.ts:textLevelOf`, `core/view/status-page-parts.ts:foldLevelOf`, `hooks/pane/palettes.ts`, `codex/src/tab-css/base.css`, `herdr/src/paint-theme.ts:faintOf` |
| Supporting text (`supporting-text`) | An open question's or task's Holds up line, its context line, and the note and recommendation under each answer option draw in `pal.supporting`, a step above muted: `SUPPORTING_TEXT`, muted moved `SUPPORTING_TEXT_SHARE`, 45%, of the way toward the theme's text color, `#d2d2d2` in dark and `#3d3d3d` in light. The light-daltonized palette moves its own muted. The ANSI themes, and a theme the mod does not know, draw it in `inactive`, as muted. The Holds up line a collapsed row draws under its title, the earlier answer and the file line stay muted. | The same lines draw in `inactive`, as muted. | `--supporting`: the text color mixed into the host's muted, `--supporting-share` of the way, which `codex/build.mjs` writes from `SUPPORTING_TEXT_SHARE`. | A theme key cannot be mixed. The desktop app resolves a color the same in both its themes, and a translucent color over a lighter page always lands lighter, so no one color sits between muted and text on both pages. Codex's muted is translucent, and `color-mix` in sRGB mixes the colors it composites, so the mix lands where `supportingOver` puts it. | `core/view/tones.ts:supportingOver`, `core/view/row-parts.ts:isSupportingPart`, `hooks/pane/palettes.ts`, `hooks/pane/pane-ask-tree.tsx`, `hooks/pane/pane-desktop-card.tsx`, `codex/src/tab-css/base.css`, `codex/src/tab-ask-tree.tsx`, `herdr/src/paint-theme.ts:supportingOf`, `herdr/src/paint-blocks.ts:bodyLineEl` |
