# Differences on purpose: the Status goal's buttons and field

Part of [the look map](../looks.md#differences-on-purpose). The reply field and the other buttons are in [Keys, clicks and replies](differences-input.md).

| Topic | Terminal | Desktop | Codex | Why | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The goal's buttons on its heading row (`status-goal`) | Keyed Buttons after the heading's `─` rule, which the mod sizes so the last button ends at the list's right edge. When they do not fit beside the title with a 4-cell rule, the rule runs to the edge and the buttons draw on the next line, after a growing spacer. | Native Buttons after a hairline Box with `flexGrow`, so they sit at the frame's right edge. A desktop Button is as small as its label and the pane has no `justifyContent` the docs mark verified, so a growing Box pushes them right, as it does the rule on Other work's heading. When the title, a 4-cell rule and the buttons' measured points do not fit, the buttons draw on their own row after a growing spacer Box. | Row buttons after a flex rule; the row wraps and `margin-left: auto` keeps them at the right on the next line. | Each look reaches the right edge with what its host has. omp pads the line to the panel's width and moves the buttons to a line of their own, right-aligned, when they do not fit. | `hooks/pane/pane-status-goal.tsx:goalHeading`, `codex/src/tab-status.tsx:GoalSection`, `omp/src/panel-goal.ts:headingLines` |
| The goal field while its press is out | The field closes at once, and the new goal shows in the same draw. | Same as terminal. | The field stays open, its buttons dimmed, until the server confirms the goal. A failed press shows why under it. | The mod runs the goal press in its own process before the next draw. The Codex tab sends it to its server, so closing the field first would show the old goal until the answer comes. omp closes the field at once, as the terminal does. | `hooks/pane/pane-status.tsx`, `codex/src/tab-status.tsx:GoalField` |
| The goal field's buttons | Save is the field's own submit, drawn `⏎ save` beside the terminal field. Cancel draws on a row under the field. | Same as terminal, with Save as the field's native submit. | Save and Cancel sit on the field's line, and wrap under it in a narrow tab. | A Codex text field carries its buttons on its own line, as the reply field does. omp draws the placeholder as a dim label over the field, and its footer reads `↵ Save · esc Cancel`, as for its reply field. | `hooks/pane/pane-status-goal-field.tsx:fieldKeys`, `codex/src/tab-status.tsx:GoalField`, `omp/src/panel-goal.ts:fieldLines`, `omp/src/panel-tabs.ts:footer` |
| The goal field's height | One row while the goal fits on a line. Once it wraps or takes a new line, the field is 3 rows tall. Past that the text scrolls in the field, and the cursor's row stays in view. | One row while the goal fits on a line. The field grows as the text wraps or takes a new line, up to 2 rows. Past that the text scrolls in the field. | A one-line field that never grows. A longer goal scrolls sideways. | The engine draws an Input as many rows tall as its `rows`, whatever it holds, so the mod counts the rows from the draft. A goal is usually one line. Each look's cap is its reply field's. The terminal field's Box is the pane's width, and it takes 1 row or 3, for the engine's reasons that the reply field's row gives. The terminal counts whether the goal fits on one row at the list's width, less the `⏎ save` the engine draws beside the field. Desktop counts them at the Input's 180 pt, a width it keeps whatever room the pane gives. A Codex `<input>` holds one line. omp draws the field as lines wrapped to the panel, as many as the draft takes. | `hooks/pane/pane-status-goal-field.tsx:fieldInput`, `hooks/pane/pane-sizes.ts:fieldRows`, `codex/src/tab-status.tsx:GoalField`, `omp/src/panel-goal.ts:fieldLines` |
| Quiet buttons, such as the goal's New | Drawn like any button: a plain terminal Button has no quieter style. | Drawn like any native Button. | A ghost button, pulled left by its side padding so its label starts at the text edge, as the Herdr panel's quiet buttons do. | The emphasis is the shared plan's; each look draws it with what its host has. omp draws a quiet button dim until it is focused. | `core/view/status-keys.ts:goalKeyPlan` |
