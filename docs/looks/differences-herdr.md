# Differences on purpose: the Herdr look

Part of [the look map](../looks.md#differences-on-purpose).

This table compares the Herdr look with the Codex tab, the nearest look. The structure check reads the parts whose names start with `differences-herdr` as the Herdr look's table.

| Topic | Herdr look | Why | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A collapsed row the cursor rests on | Keeps the selected row's background, without its body and buttons. A click on the open row's title collapses it, and a click on a collapsed row expands it. The Codex tab draws the plain closed line. | `z` acts on the cursor row, so the panel keeps it visible. | `herdr/src/paint-blocks.ts:rowEl` |
| Who draws | A Herdr app mod draws the inbox apart from the host, from the session file the host publishes under the app's home folder. | Herdr's mods run in Herdr's own process, so the look reaches the host's session only through a file. | `herdr/src/files.ts:readSession`, `core/remote/herdr.ts:publishedSessionPath` |
| Following focus | Shows the session of the terminal last focused in the look's tab, and keeps it while the look itself has focus. Before anything in the tab had focus, it shows the first terminal with a session label. A reply typed for one session waits with that session while the look follows another, and its row's Reply opens it again when the look comes back. | One look pane can serve several agents in a tab. | `herdr/src/follow.ts:follow`, `herdr/src/main.ts` |
| A terminal with no session | Draws a message in place of the tabs: no terminal in the tab, no inbox in the terminal with the program that runs there, or a session that ended. | The followed terminal can be any program, and a session can end while its terminal stays open. | `herdr/src/screen.ts:screenOf` |
| Presses | The look writes each press to a file under the app's home folder, then sets a label on the terminal the session file names, so the host reads it. While a row has a press out, every press button on it draws out. A Mark press, such as Dismiss, Done, an answer, or Set goal and Remove on the goal, draws nothing more until its reply, and the row keeps the line it drew before, as `holdsStatusWhileOut` says. The Codex tab shows "Sending…" in an ask row's side column meanwhile. For any other press the row reads what its opens, copies or refreshes read while under way, such as "Refreshing PRs…", or "Sending…", or how the look's own opens went. While the press is out, that line goes where `isStatusUnderActions` puts it, even once the look's own open is done. A status line the row drew above its buttons, such as "Still open", stays there while a press under way puts the row's status under them, so the buttons keep their place, by the shared rule the Codex tab holds its status after the title with (`core/view/held-line.ts:stepHeldLine`). A failure line keeps its space there without its words while the press is under way, since the press retries what it says failed. The line stays after the press too, while the row's status stays under its buttons, as with "Not confirmed". A status that belongs above takes its place. Otherwise the hold lets go once no press is under way: the line fades out with its words while its space holds, then its space closes with the row's box, so the place never stays empty. A held line that ends with its age, as a PR row's does, keeps counting it. A row that drew no line above its buttons when its press went out keeps its done status, such as "✓ Discussed", under them until the row closes or loses its buttons. A row that settles holds nothing from its open form, since Undo takes the place of its buttons. Its box shrinks to the settled height, and Undo sits under the settled line. The panel passes whether the row shows buttons, where the other looks pass whether it is open, so a settled row's press status also goes under its Undo. A press ends when a publish names it as the last press the host handled, or shows its row changed. A press the host found already changed gets the shared stale note. A press a host took reads as not confirmed when the host's last press is a later one, or 15 seconds after its file was written. One that no host took by then, the look takes back out of the folder, so it cannot apply later, and it reads as not applied. A press on a row with a press out, or with no session to send to, is not sent, and its row says why. | The look reaches the host only through files and terminal labels, so a press waits for the next publish. The panel draws a settled row's Undo as a button under its line, so a status above it would move it while its press is out. The panel draws a status that follows the title on its own line, where the other looks put one that is not a failure in the title's text. Dropping that line at a press, or adding one when it ends, would move the buttons. A host's apply can fail without a publish, so the look cannot know more than that the press was taken. The panel's age takes only its own width beside the title, where the Codex tab reserves a side column as wide as "Sending…" on every row. "Sending…" there would wrap the title again, and reserving its width would narrow every title in a narrow pane, for a sign the drawn-out buttons already give. | `herdr/src/press-queue.ts:PressQueue`, `core/view/feedback.ts:holdsStatusWhileOut`, `herdr/src/out.ts:settleOuts`, `herdr/src/out.ts:drawOuts`, `herdr/src/held-line.ts:holdLinesAbove`, `herdr/src/paint-blocks.ts:rowEl` |
| Reply while its send is out | The draft stays through the press and until the reply arrives, by the shared controller's rule: the row's last action is newer than the press, its reply is the sent text, and its delivery is neither held, queued, failed nor unconfirmed. Reply pressed meanwhile opens with the sent words. A refused or stale reply reopens its field when the person has not moved since the send. The terminal and desktop looks delete the draft once the press is confirmed. | The Herdr look runs the shared controller, which `docs/plans/ui-controller.md` moves each look onto. The look learns how a press went only from the session the host publishes, so only waiting for the reply keeps the draft past the press. | `core/view/ui-controller-reply.ts:releasedDrafts`, `herdr/src/main.ts:Inbox`, `herdr/src/press-queue.ts:PressQueue` |
| Opens and copies | The look runs every copy, and every open of a link, an absolute path or a `~/` path, itself at once, and its row shows how it went. The press file carries what happened. It opens a relative path against the project folder the session file names, and sends it to the host when the file names none. | The person sees the result without waiting for a publish. A host leaves the project folder out when it has no absolute one. | `herdr/src/run-here.ts:isRunHere`, `herdr/src/run-here.ts:runHere` |
| A spent Undo | Once Undo ends, a settled row that offered it keeps Undo in its place until it folds, drawn as quiet as a button whose press is out, taking no press. While the row's Undo press is still out, Undo stays drawn out there, since the shared plan drops it when its time ends. A row whose message is still queued `QUEUED_AFTER_MS` after Undo ended waits for the agent, so its spent Undo fades out then while its place holds, and then its place closes. The look draws again at each of those moments. A PR's block keeps no place for a spent Undo. | The panel draws Undo on a line of its own under the row's status, where the Codex tab draws it beside the title. A hidden Undo would leave an empty line, and a row that waits for the agent can stay for minutes. The host sends an answer or Fix held for Undo when Undo ends, so a row the agent takes at once still folds in one move. | `herdr/src/draw.ts:undoButtons`, `core/view/ui-controller-clock.ts:undoPlaceEndsAt`, `herdr/src/out.ts:drawOuts`, `herdr/src/screen.ts:screenOf` |
| Tab widths | A tab whose count widens, or that gains or loses a count, takes its new width at once, and the tabs after it move sideways in the same draw. The Codex tab glides them. | gpui-kit animates only values the mod sets, and a tab's width is its text's width, which the mod does not set. | `herdr/src/paint-chrome.ts:tabsEl` |
| Running marks (`running-marks`) | A running mark keeps its row's color and fades from 1 to 0.3 and back every `RUNNING_PULSE_MS`, as in the Codex tab. It always fades. | The Herdr app tells a mod no reduced-motion setting. | `herdr/src/paint-parts.ts:markEl` |
| Help | A circled question mark in a tab at the right end of the tab row, opposite the four tabs, opens Help in place of the tab. While Help shows, that tab is the selected one and the four tabs are not, and a press on it or on any tab leaves Help. Help lists the keys, then the marks, and no tab symbols. The inbox build the host published sits at the right end of the Keys title, in the theme's code font. A key or a mark sits in the left column and its words beside it, in a column every line shares. | Herdr draws the pane's header from the layout's header mod, and a mod pane cannot add a button to it. A footer would cost a row. The look always names its tabs in words. | `herdr/src/draw-help.ts:helpBlocks`, `herdr/src/paint-chrome.ts:tabsEl` |
| PR titles | A PR's title wraps in its own column, with its number muted at the line's right end. In a pane under 280 px, the number leads the line under the title instead. Where the PR stands, with its reasons, goes on the line under the title. Each of its parts moves whole to the next line when it does not fit, so a wrapped line starts at the edge. The Codex tab runs the number, the title and where the PR stands as one paragraph. | gpui-kit has no inline text span, so the title cannot run into the next text the way a paragraph does, and the status line cannot wrap as one paragraph. A number at the right end lines up like an age without tabular figures, which gpui-kit lacks. | `herdr/src/paint-blocks.ts:prEl` |
| Type and layout | Every gap, padding and inset comes from the shared scale in `core/view/layout.ts`, a 4 pt unit, as every look's does. The panel keeps `EDGE_PT` (16) on each side, and the tab row, banners, PR titles, ages, Help's section titles and rule ends sit on it. A mark sits at the start of a `MARK_PT` (24) column that begins at that edge, so text after a mark starts at 40 px. A Finds group's heading starts there too, after its mark, in line with the rows under it. A heading with no mark starts at the edge. Questions and Tasks take a mark like Finds' groups do. Help lists keys and marks in one pair of columns: a key or a mark starts 24 px in from the edge (`HELP_INDENT`, one nested step), under its section's title, and its words start 36 px later (`HELP_COLUMN`: the widest key, about 28 px and allowed 32, plus 8 px), at 80 px. The Status tab's goal, head, Later and Other work headings start at the edge. Text with no marks under it, such as a PR and the PR order, starts at the edge. A nested item's marks start where its parent's text does, 24 px in. A PR's threads and checks hang their marks at the edge under its title. A title's detail sits on the line under it, with no gap. Buttons sit `CONTROL_GAP_PT` (8) under the text above them, and a press's result sits `CONTROL_GAP_PT` under the buttons it follows. Titles sit `LIST_MARGIN_PT` (4) above row boxes, Closed folds as far below, other headings `HEAD_GAP_PT` (8). Two one-line Status items sit `LIST_GAP_PT` (4) apart. Status items with more than one line sit `ITEM_GAP_PT` (12) apart at the top level and `CHILD_GAP_PT` (8) apart under one parent. A question's Holds up line, context and earlier answer sit `CHILD_GAP_PT` apart, the first directly under the title. Answers that start the tree sit `CONTROL_GAP_PT` under the title, as buttons under any text (`titleToBodyGapPt`). Options sit `ITEM_GAP_PT` apart. A note sits `LIST_GAP_PT` under its button, from its label (`BUTTON_LABEL_X`). A row or other fold pads `ROW_PAD_PT` (6) inside its box, closed or open, so two rows sit 12 px apart with their boxes touching. Sections sit `SECTION_GAP_PT` (24) apart, with a rule, where there is one, in the middle. The tab row keeps 8 px under its tabs. The list starts 16 px below that, so its first block sits 24 px under the tabs, and it ends with 32 px under its last block. A banner or the session note sits 24 px under the tabs too, and 16 px under a banner above it, where the shared `BANNER_GAP_PT` gives the other looks 8. A fold's label starts at 40 px, with its chevron 4 px after it. Headings use a plain " · ". A line of dot-joined parts, such as a PR's reasons, a fold's note, a thread's place and author, or Other work's counts, centers each dot in a 9.5 px slot, the width a text dot takes, which is hidden where its part starts a line (`herdr/src/paint-parted.ts:partedEl`). Buttons and tabs are 28 px high, with 8 px between two buttons and between two wrapped lines of them. Quiet buttons have no side padding, and the goal's Edit and Remove have no dot between them. | Pete picked the 40 px text column from the three systems in the panel's type study, and the 4 pt scale from the spacing plan's three. He asked for the tab row on the panel's own padding, for no empty mark column on the PRs tab or beside the Status tab's goal, and for margins in proportion across the tabs. A banner takes the panel's 16 px between banners so it lines up with the list under it. | `core/view/layout.ts`, `herdr/src/paint-theme.ts`, `herdr/src/paint-space.ts:gapBetween`, `herdr/src/paint-blocks.ts`, `herdr/src/paint-text.ts:feedbackEl`, `herdr/src/paint-chrome.ts:tabsEl` |
| Scroll edges | The list fades into the pane's background over its top 16 px and its bottom 32 px. The fades always draw. At rest they lie over the list's empty padding, so they show only where rows scroll under an edge. The Codex tab draws no fade. | gpui-kit cannot tell a mod how far a list has scrolled, so a fade cannot switch on and off with the scroll. A mod has no CSS, so it draws each fade as a gradient path. | `herdr/src/paint-chrome.ts:scrollFadeEl` |
| A press that is out (`dim-pressed-button`) | A button whose press is out keeps its size, loses its fill and border, and draws its label muted at half strength. It shows no hover or pressed change and takes no click. The Codex tab draws the whole button at 40% opacity, so a filled button that is out reads as a grey block. | A filled button at 40% opacity drew the eye away from the buttons that still work. | `herdr/src/paint-parts.ts:buttonEl` |
| A row's title tint (`title-tint`) | The row's title and mark take the theme's blue on a new row, or the tone of how it closed on a settled row, at `TITLE_TINT` of its strength, then steps back to its own color in `NEW_STEPS` colors, each mixed into the title's color as one opaque color. The look draws again at each step. Reduced motion is not read. | GPUI glides no text color and its types expose no reduced motion setting. | `herdr/src/paint-title-tint.ts:titleColorOf`, `core/view/ui-controller-tabs.ts:newRowsOf` |
| A font weight change | The selected tab and the name of a top-level milestone that is not done take `font_semibold` in one draw. The Herdr app draws every weight the same today, so none of them looks different from regular text. | In Geist, the Herdr app's UI font, no weight draws: `font_semibold()`, `font_bold()` and `font_weight(n)` for 400 to 700 all measured 346 px wide with the same ink in a 3024 px capture. Naming `Geist` as the family changes nothing. Weight draws in fonts that have the face, such as Helvetica Neue, Menlo and the system UI font, but their letterforms differ from Geist's, so the look does not switch family. Filed as [herdrdev/herdr#6037](https://github.com/herdrdev/herdr/issues/6037). So the look has no weight to show and none to step on its frame timer, as it steps a title's tint. Filed as [herdrdev/herdr#6037](https://github.com/herdrdev/herdr/issues/6037). `transition` and `spring` also take only `opacity`, `width`, `height`, `left` and `top`. | `herdr/src/paint-chrome.ts:tabsEl`, `herdr/src/paint-blocks.ts` |
| A question's Holds up line (`asks-holds-up-line`) | Draws it only on the open row, as the first branch of the row's tree (`asks-tree`). A collapsed row draws only its title, so its Holds up line does not show. | The Codex tab, the terminal and omp draw a collapsed row's Holds up line plain under its title. The Herdr pane draws a row's body only while the row is open, so the body comes and goes with the panel, whose height glides. | `herdr/src/draw.ts:rowBody` |
