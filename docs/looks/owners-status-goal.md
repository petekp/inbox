# Owners: the Status goal

Which file owns each rule about the Status page's goal, its buttons and field, and the Other work part. The rest of the page is in [The Status page](owners-status.md).

| Rule | Owner | Read by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The goal (`status-goal`): the goal the Status call set or the person typed, its milestones, and the Now line | `hooks/status-goal-page.ts:goalPage` builds `StatusPage.goal`, with its label, `GOAL_HEADING` or "Goal met", and its Now line, `GoalBlock.now`, left out when `hooks/status-path.ts:namesOnlyMilestoneUnderWay` finds it only names the milestone under way. `hooks/status-path.ts:milestonePath` orders the milestones as a path: done ones, those under way, the next queued one, then Later. It writes each one's line. A NEXT line from the Status call picks which queued milestone is next. `hooks/status-page.ts:statusPage` fills each milestone's `rows`. `hooks/status-goal-ops.ts` applies GOAL, RETIRE, MILESTONE and SERVES lines, and `hooks/status-goal-gate.ts` says when code takes a GOAL or RETIRE line. A GOAL line sets the goal while none is set, once the goal is met, or over an inferred goal none of whose work under an open milestone is open, and only while an open subject sits outside the current goal. It also replaces an inferred goal whose work is open when it revises that goal, as `goalRevision` says: it cuts a milestone that was open and not finished, by leaving it out or naming it dropped, names again with an open status one that holds open work, and the same answer's SERVES lines send each other open subject the goal holds to - or to a milestone it names again. A revision that leaves open work with no place is refused, and `goalRefused.unplaced` names that work. A milestone named again keeps its open work and asks, and, while its status is the same, its text. A refused line is saved as `goalRefused`, which only the next Status call reads. A typed goal stays until it is met. A goal that still waits for its milestones is not met, even when the milestones it carries from the goal before it are all done. A GOAL line restating a typed goal just set, from a call that read it, gives that goal its milestones. `settleGoal` closes each milestone whose linked work closed, and sets when the goal is met: every milestone left is done. A milestone is held, ◆ with no word, while an open ask links to it or to a subject that serves it. Once the goal is met, `goalPage` sets `GoalBlock.mark` to the done ✓ and drops the Now line. `statusPage` then sets `GoalBlock.milestonesFold` with `hooks/status-goal-page.ts:metMilestonesFold`, once every milestone is done and nothing under them is open, held or running, seen or not. The fold line names the done milestones with `hooks/status-page-parts.ts:namedList`. Its id names the goal and when it was met, so a fold opened for one met goal starts shut for the next, and for the same goal met again. `hooks/status-goal-page.ts:shownMilestones` leaves the milestones out until the person opens that fold, which each look keeps as it keeps a done fold's. | All four looks draw the goal's section first on every page that is not empty, in the same shape with a goal or without one. Its heading row holds the goal's label, or `GOAL_HEADING` while no goal is set, muted, at the goal text's column, then a solid rule, then the goal's buttons at the right edge, a dim `STALL_GAP` (` · `) between each two. On desktop the dot is a dim Text between the native Buttons. When the buttons do not fit beside the title, the rule runs to the edge and they wrap to the next line, still at the right, never cut. Then the goal's text, or `NO_GOAL_TEXT`, muted, while no goal is set. Then its Prev line and its Now line, muted, in place of the head, then the milestones. A milestone's rows start on the line after its name or its text, as a subject's children do. A milestone that draws only its name, such as a done one folded to its name, has the next milestone on the adjacent line. After a milestone that draws more, a space comes before the next milestone or the Later label, so a milestone's work sits closer to its own name than to the next (`hooks/status-path.ts:isSpacedAbove`). The terminal and omp draw a blank line there, desktop and Codex `hooks/layout.ts:STATUS_ROW_PAD_PT` more than between two rows, and Herdr its `ITEM_GAP`, where two milestones' lines sit `CONTROL_GAP` apart and a name its `INSET` above its first row. The space under the goal's text stays a section's. A milestone draws like a subject row, with its rows under it one step in. Its rows join it as a subject's children do. The nesting connector runs down from under its mark, each row it reaches branches off it, and it ends in an elbow at its last row one step in. Where the path's line runs on past the milestone, the path's line is that column, and each row one step in branches off it. A vertical line runs down the marks' column from the first milestone on the path to the next one, dashed or `┆` where it runs into next. The "Later" label and its rule sit above the first milestone after the path, and those milestones are muted. A milestone changed since the last look draws its name bold. A done milestone the person has seen draws only its name, muted, and its rows fold behind the fold mark after the name (`hooks/status-folds.ts:milestoneRows`). While no goal is set, or once the goal is met, the head is the heading of the list after the goal (see Other work), so it never reads as the goal. Once the goal is met, its ✓ hangs in the mark column beside "Goal met", which takes the done tone, and the goal's text and Prev line start at the text column, as does the field New opens. Under them, the milestones fold into one line at the text column that names them, with the fold mark after, as a done milestone folded to its name draws. A press opens it to the milestones' path. Nothing else in the met goal gains a check. |
| The goal's buttons and its text field | `hooks/status-keys.ts:goalKeyPlan` gives New, after Undo while a removed goal can come back, then Edit while a goal is set or New once the goal is met, then Remove. The labels leave out "goal", since they sit on the Goal heading. Keys: `n` New, `e` Edit, `r` Remove, `u` Undo. `hooks/status-keys.ts:statusKeyRows` puts them first in the order j and k move, then a met goal's folded milestones, with `f`, then the milestones it shows, as the goal's section comes first. `hooks/goal-press.ts:goalPressed` runs Remove, its Undo and a typed goal through `hooks/pressed.ts:pressed`, on the id `goal`. None sends a message. | Every look draws the plan's buttons in order on the goal's heading row. Edit and New open the look's reply field under the draft id `goal`, with the plan's text in it. While the field is open, it takes the place of the goal's text, or of the line that says no goal is set, the buttons leave the heading, and no Status target is selected or has keys. On the empty page, New and its field draw under the empty state's text. Save, the field's submit, runs a `type` press on `goal`. Codex keeps the field open, its buttons dimmed, until the server confirms the goal, and shows why a goal press failed. `hooks/pane-status-goal.tsx`, `codex/src/tab-status.tsx` and the omp panel draw them. |
| Other work, and the head | While a goal is set, `hooks/status-page.ts:statusPage` lists under a `part` row, "Other work", the subjects whose SERVES is `none` and, while it shows milestones, every subject no milestone serves. Running work under no milestone comes first. Seen open subjects the goal does not need, unless held or running, fold into one `counts` row after its open rows, a count for working and for waiting on someone else (`hooks/status-page-parts.ts:countsByMark`). Seen queued ones leave the page. Its subjects the goal needs fold into its own "○ N more" before the counts. A row or part that would show nothing is left out. While no goal is set, and once the goal is met, the work no longer serves the goal, so the head is the list's first `part` row (`isTitle`, id `head`): its title in place of "Other work", and its description, which the Status call may not have written yet, on the line under it. The goal then draws no Now line. With a met goal whose milestones are folded, the list can show the head and then "Other work". Seen done ones fold into Other work's own done fold at its end. | All four looks draw the `part` row as its name and a rule, the head's title bold in the text color and its description muted under the rule at the text column, with a blank line above it in the terminal and omp, and `hooks/layout.ts:SECTION_GAP_PT` on desktop and in Codex, except at the top of the list (see Differences on purpose). Each look draws the `counts` row as each mark in its tone with its count, joined by " · ". The counts wrap where they do not fit. |
