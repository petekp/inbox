# Files: the Herdr look

Part of [the look map](../looks.md#files).

The Herdr look is a Herdr app mod. It draws the inbox of the session that a host publishes for the terminal it follows. `herdr/build.ts` bundles `herdr/src` and the shared modules it imports into `herdr/mod/inbox/`, the folder an install copies into the app's `mods` folder.

| File | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `herdr/src/main.ts` | Herdr adapter. The only file that imports `herdr`, the Herdr app's state and effects. It and the `herdr/src/paint-*.ts` files are the only ones that import `gpui-kit` and `gpui-base`, since painting does not fit one file under the size limit. The other files decide what to draw, so they stay testable without Herdr. The mod's `View`: it follows the terminal, watches and reads the session's file, keeps the shared controller's state for each session, runs the commands it returns, wakes at its `nextWakeAt`, keeps each block's measured height, and writes presses. |
| `herdr/src/controller.ts` | Herdr adapter. Turns the look's clicks and buttons into the shared controller's events, applies them with `reduceUi`, and turns the controller's state into what the drawing reads. Keeps the controller's state of each session the look left. |
| `herdr/src/follow.ts` | Herdr adapter. Which terminal in the look's tab it follows, from Herdr's focus, panes and terminal labels, and whether that terminal shows a session, no inbox, or an ended session. It imports no Herdr module. |
| `herdr/src/files.ts` | Herdr adapter. Which changes `watchFolder` reports can change the session's file, at the path `core/remote/herdr.ts:publishedSessionPath` gives, and reading that file: missing, torn by a read mid-write, from a newer host, refused, or read with its session upgraded, with a torn read read again. |
| `herdr/src/screen.ts` | Herdr adapter. What one draw shows: the message for each case of the followed terminal, or the inbox's tabs, banners and blocks. |
| `herdr/src/blocks.ts` | Herdr adapter. The blocks the paint draws: headings, rows, Status lines, PRs, text fields, folds, notes and rules, with the tones the paint maps onto Herdr's theme. Each button says what a press does: change the view, send a press, open a text field, or send or leave one. |
| `herdr/src/draw.ts` | Herdr adapter. Asks and Finds as blocks, from the shared view and `rowKeyPlan`: groups, rows with their buttons in the plan's order, a settled row's Undo from its `undo` action, and Closed folds. Also the tab cells and banners. |
| `herdr/src/wake.ts` | Herdr adapter. `nextDrawAt`: when the look next redraws on its own, for the next frame of a move, a fold or an Undo ending, or a fading bar. |
| `herdr/src/draw-status.ts` | Herdr adapter. The Status page as blocks, from the shared page and its key plans: the goal, milestones, rows, folds and rules, and the goal's text field. |
| `herdr/src/draw-prs.ts` | Herdr adapter. The PRs tab as blocks, from `threadActions`, `checkActions`, `prBlockActions` and `prClearActions`: each PR with its threads and checks, the refresh, clear and order lines, and the empty and `gh` notes. |
| `herdr/src/draw-help.ts` | Herdr adapter. Help as blocks: the keys from `helpKeys` and the marks from `MARK_LEGEND`. |
| `herdr/src/keys.ts` | Herdr adapter. What a key does, as a shared controller event or a button press: change tab, move the selection, collapse or expand the selected row, press the last close's Undo, leave the pane, close the open text field, or press the selected row's button with that key. Whether the text field is drawn. |
| `herdr/src/held-line.ts` | Herdr adapter. Keeps the status line a row drew above its buttons while its status goes under them, during a press and after it, so the buttons hold their place. Keeps a press's status under the buttons of a row that drew no line above them. Recounts a held line's age. |
| `herdr/src/under-line.ts` | Herdr adapter. Steps the status under a row's buttons, such as "Sending…", as it comes and goes. A status a press brings shows in the press's draw, and one a press takes away goes at once. Any other status fades in once the row's box is nearly open, and fades out while the box holds its height. |
| `herdr/src/motion.ts` | Herdr adapter. Steps each drawn block through the shared row states in `core/view/row-motion.ts`, keeps a block the publish dropped drawn while it leaves, says whether its box is open or shut in this draw, when a closing row's box leaves out its panel, and how a row's title moves while the row opens or closes. A block that leaves stays drawn until its fade ends and its height spring comes to rest. |
| `herdr/src/moved-blocks.ts` | Herdr adapter. Which blocks changed place between two draws: the fewest whose removal leaves the rest in their drawn order, so `motion.ts` moves only those. |
| `herdr/src/panel-steps.ts` | Herdr adapter. The spring of a row's panel space as the look sets its target, and when each step of a turned-back panel's fade starts, from the steps `turnBackFades` in `core/view/row-motion.ts` gives for where that space is, or of a faint panel's close, from `faintCloseFades`. The opacity those steps draw at a given time. |
| `herdr/src/block-glide.ts` | Herdr adapter. How a block's box glides as it enters or leaves: whether the box is open or shut in this draw, whether its content shows and how long it fades, when an entering box opens if a leaving block holds its space, and when a leaving box starts to shut. |
| `herdr/src/heights.ts` | Herdr adapter. The size each list block and row panel last measured, kept after a block leaves. Sizes are kept at zoom 1, the scale the look sets sizes in: the app's size callback reports a size its zoom has already scaled, so each is divided by the zoom from `settings()` as it is saved. A change of zoom draws again. Says when a height changed, so the look draws again, and which boxes changed height with their width, so they take their new height at once. |
| `herdr/src/out.ts` | Herdr adapter. The presses the look sent that have no result yet: which buttons draw out, what their rows read, and when a read of the session ends each one. |
| `herdr/src/press-queue.ts` | Herdr adapter. One session's presses from send to end: refusing a press, running the look's own opens, writing the press file, waking the host, and taking back a file no host took in time. Its effects come through `QueueIo`, so the tests run it without the app. |
| `herdr/src/press-file.ts` | Herdr adapter. Where the look writes a press for the host, the file's text in the format `core/remote/herdr-press-file.ts:pressFile` reads, and sets the label that wakes the host. |
| `herdr/src/collapse.ts` | Herdr adapter. Writes a collapse file for the host, and draws the look's own collapses until the session it reads shows them. |
| `herdr/src/fold-all.ts` | Herdr adapter. `foldAllIn` applies a fold-all to the look's folds and saves each collapse through the look's `collapse`. `foldClickOpens` says whether an Option-click on a fold opens every fold or folds them all. |
| `herdr/src/status-look.ts` | Herdr adapter. Whether the Herdr app shows the look's pane, where the Status rows sit from their measured heights, which rows are surely on screen wherever the list is scrolled, the look at the Status under way, and the look file it writes for the host when the look ends. |
| `herdr/src/look-reports.ts` | Herdr adapter. What the panel tells a session's host about its use: the looks at the Status, through `status-look.ts`, and a report, on opening the PRs tab and every 30 seconds while it is shown, that the PRs tab is shown. A host that gets it reads the session's PRs if they are due, as it does for a tab it shows itself. |
| `herdr/src/io.ts` | Herdr adapter. Runs commands, probes paths, writes press files and wakes the host, through the Herdr calls `main.ts` passes in. |
| `herdr/src/run-here.ts` | Herdr adapter. Which opens and copies the look runs itself, at once, and running them. |
| `herdr/src/paint-theme.ts` | Herdr adapter. Maps the blocks' tones onto Herdr's theme, the text sizes, the spacing scale and column positions the paint files share, and what they share for one draw, with whether the pane is narrow and the list's room, from the sizes the app reported, at zoom 1. |
| `herdr/src/paint-space.ts` | Herdr adapter. The space above each block, from what comes before it, so every distance in the list takes a step of the scale. Puts each block in a box that keeps its height when the list overflows. Inside it, the space above the block and the block's content each sit in a box whose height springs, the content's at its measured height. A block that enters or leaves springs both to or from none and fades, and the blocks around it take their space from the last block that is not shut. A row's height is its title's and the rest's, each measured on its own. The tab bar and the banners above the list sit in a box that springs the same way. A row's title sits in a box at its closed form's height, up to two lines, unless the row is open or its box holds the taller form's height while its title moves. |
| `herdr/src/paint-strokes.ts` | Herdr adapter. Paints a tree's columns and arms in each block, and which fade each part takes: a part that joins a block that comes or goes fades with that block, and a column between blocks that stay does not fade. |
| `herdr/src/form-swap.ts` | Herdr adapter. A row's change between its settled form and another, as it settles or as Undo brings it back: the old form's lines under the title fade out while the row's height holds, then the new form's lines fade in once its box moves. |
| `herdr/src/title-motion.ts` | Herdr adapter. When a row's title fades between a short form and a tall form, so the fade runs while the box rests and the box never cuts a line of the title that shows. The forms are the closed title and the whole title while the row opens or closes, and a settled row's one line and the collapsed title's two-line cut while the row settles or comes back through Undo. |
| `herdr/src/paint-title-text.ts` | Herdr adapter. Paints a row's title text, both forms at once while the title moves, and the hidden copies `title-cut.ts` measures. |
| `herdr/src/title-cut.ts` | Herdr adapter. A collapsed row's title on two lines, cut at a word: which copies of the title to measure hidden, and the cut those sizes give. A settled row's title and a Closed line's take one line. |
| `herdr/src/paint-checkbox.ts` | Herdr adapter. The Tasks mark, a checked box drawn as a shape, since the font's ☑ is small beside the other marks. |
| `herdr/src/paint-chevron.ts` | Herdr adapter. A fold's chevron, drawn as a path from the Codex tab's chevron icon, turned down when open, with the count of the rows a folded parent hides. A bright copy lies over it and shows on hover, since the kit styles an element only on its own hover. |
| `herdr/src/paint-blocks.ts` | Herdr adapter. Paints each block with `gpui-kit` and `gpui-base`: rows, with a leaving block's buttons, text field and fold inert, text fields, Status lines, PRs, headings, folds, rules, notes, empty states and banner blocks. |
| `herdr/src/paint-parted.ts` | Herdr adapter. `partedEl`: parts joined by a dot, wrapping whole, with the dot of a part that starts a line hidden. |
| `herdr/src/paint-field.ts` | Herdr adapter. A text field with its buttons under it, for a row's reply field and any other field block. |
| `herdr/src/paint-status-line.ts` | Herdr adapter. A Status line and a Help line: the mark or key, the name, its stall, fold chevron and buttons, and the parts a line is built from. |
| `herdr/src/paint-empty.ts` | Herdr adapter. An empty tab's block: its title, hint and any line, buttons, field or feedback under them, centred across and down the list's room. |
| `herdr/src/paint-tree.ts` | Herdr adapter. Paints the parts of a row's tree: its trunk and arms, and its answers' buttons, stacked or side by side, with the test of whether they fit side by side. |
| `herdr/src/paint-parts.ts` | Herdr adapter. The small parts every block draws with: a button, a row of buttons, a mark, a line of text and a press's result line. |
| `herdr/src/paint-chrome.ts` | Herdr adapter. Paints the tab row with Help at its right end, the banners, the session notes, the fades at the list's scroll edges, and the message for a terminal with no inbox. Herdr's pane header names the pane, so the look draws no title. |
| `herdr/src/paint-notes.ts` | Herdr adapter. A rule's line and its optional text, and a note line, such as the session note or a strong heading line. |
| `herdr/src/paint-timer.ts` | Herdr adapter. The fill behind a timed button's label, which empties over the button's time left, as the Codex tab's Undo does. The fill is a pill of the button's shape, cut straight at the share left, so it never draws outside the button. Each draw sets the share from the button's clock, so a new element shows the right fill. A timed button keeps a fixed width, so nothing shifts while its time runs. |
| `herdr/src/paint-title-tint.ts` | Herdr adapter. The color a row's title takes while its tint lasts, and when the look draws again for it. |
| `herdr/src/time-left.ts` | Herdr adapter. A timed button's time left, as Undo shows it: the share of `SETTLED_MS` left, and whether the fill empties or pauses. It also says when the look draws next, every 250 ms while a fill empties and when a button's time runs out. The fill pauses while Undo's own press is out. It imports nothing from the Herdr app, so a test can load it alone. |
