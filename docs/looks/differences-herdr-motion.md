# Differences on purpose: the Herdr look's motion

Part of [the look map](../looks.md#differences-on-purpose).

This part of the Herdr look's table covers how blocks move. Like [the rest of the table](differences-herdr.md), it compares the Herdr look with the Codex tab.

| Topic | Herdr look | Why | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Motion | Every change of a block's height moves on a spring with no overshoot, `HEIGHT_SPRING` in `hooks/springs.ts`, and every fade runs over the shared lengths: a row opening or closing, a status line coming or going, buttons, the reply field, a PR's order lines. Each block's content sits in a box at its measured height. When the content changes, the box keeps its old height for one draw, while the size callback measures the new one, then glides to it. A row's panel, its body and buttons, fades in as it opens and out as it closes. A closing row keeps its last panel drawn without clicks while it fades, and its box leaves the panel's height out. A row's title sits in its own box, one line high unless the row is open, and the lines under the title glide with that box. As a row opens, its title wraps in the first frame and its box glides to the wrapped height. As a row closes, collapses or settles, its title drops to regular weight at once and keeps its wrap while the box glides to one line, clipping the lines under the first. The ellipsis replaces the wrap once the box rests, so only the end of the first line changes then. When the selection moves, the closing and the opening row start in the same draw, so the rows below them move once. Every block, a heading or a fold as much as a row, that leaves fades out over `LEAVE_FADE_MS` while its box holds its height, then springs shut. A block that enters springs its box open, and its content fades in over `ENTER_FADE_MS` once the box is within half of `SLIDE_PX` of open (`enterDelayMs`), as in the Codex tab. While a leaving block holds its space, a block that enters waits to open, and a row whose close starts in the same draw keeps its height while its panel fades. The person's own moves do not wait: the row the selection opens, whose panel fades in from the next frame, a press's field, which shows as soon as its box opens, and a leave turned back, which fades back in from where it is, or once its box is nearly open again if the box had begun to shut. In a draw that starts a move, a group's title and the tab bar's counts keep their last form, so they change in the draw that sets the rows moving. A leaving block stays drawn until its spring comes to rest. A row selected as it enters keeps gliding to its open height. A fold's blocks enter as it opens and leave as it shuts. A leaving block stays after the nearest block above it that is still drawn. A block that stays on the page and moves to another place leaves a copy of itself in its old place, which leaves as any block does, and enters at its new place, so the rows between the two places glide instead of jumping. The space above each block is a box of its own whose height springs. It is measured from the last block above that is not shut, so a block that enters starts with no space and a block that leaves ends with none. A move that turns back goes from where it is. The tab bar, the banners and the trimmed-session note above the list glide the list down or up as they change height: a tab bar that wraps to another line in a narrow pane, or a banner that comes or goes. Their words appear and go with the height, without a fade. A newly shown tab, Help page or session draws at rest. A box whose height changed with its width, as when the pane is resized, takes its new height at once, even mid-move. A leaving block's buttons, text field and fold stay drawn but take no clicks. A block that takes another block's id, as a dismissed PR's block after Undo, is that block in another form, so it glides between the two forms. Motion always runs. | The Herdr app draws natively and has springs of its own, and a spring eases in and out where a fixed ease-out reads as stiff. It does not overshoot, since a box clips its content and an overshoot would show blank space under its last line. Opacity keeps the shared fades, since an opacity spring could overshoot past full. A spring has no fixed length, so a block stays drawn until its spring has come to rest, which `hooks/springs.ts` computes from the spring's numbers. gpui-kit animates only a later change of its target, and only between heights the mod sets, so every box draws at a height measured with the size callback, never at `auto` during a move. A fold's shut blocks are not drawn, so they are measured in the draw that opens the fold, not before: drawing them hidden would put them in the keys and the accessibility tree. A tab or Help page is a new page with nothing in common with the last one, so it draws at once, as the Codex tab draws a new tab. A width change rewraps every block at once, and a glide would make the text lag the pane. The banners are not list blocks, so they do not keep their last words while they go. A box's spring belongs to its element id, so a block entering at its new place under the boxes it had in its old place would start from the height they were drawn at, and its boxes take new ids. A closing title drops its weight with the selection's background, so that the cut changes only the end of the first line. The Codex tab keeps a closing title semibold until its close ends. Padding cannot glide, so every distance that can change is the height of a box whose spring is declared in every draw: each space, each block's content and each row's title. A move that starts while another glides then starts where every box is drawn. A move's first draw only sets where its boxes start, so a title or count that took its new form there would change a frame before the rows move. The Codex tab draws a group's empty note inside the element that ends the group, so any height move that starts with a leave waits there, while Herdr draws the note as a block of its own that enters and waits as any entering block does. The Herdr app tells a mod no reduced-motion setting. | `herdr/src/motion.ts:moveRows`, `herdr/src/paint-space.ts:spacedEls`, `herdr/src/paint-space.ts:fitEl`, `herdr/src/paint-blocks.ts:rowEl`, `herdr/src/heights.ts:Heights`, `hooks/springs.ts:HEIGHT_SPRING` |
