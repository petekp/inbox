# Differences on purpose: the Herdr look's motion

Part of [the look map](../looks.md#differences-on-purpose).

This part of the Herdr look's table covers how blocks move. Like [the rest of the table](differences-herdr.md), it compares the Herdr look with the Codex tab.

| Topic | Herdr look | Why | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Motion | Every change of a block's height moves on a spring with no overshoot, `HEIGHT_SPRING` in `core/view/springs.ts`, and every fade runs over the shared lengths: a row opening or closing, a status line coming or going, buttons, the reply field, a PR's order lines. Each block's content sits in a box at its measured height. When the content changes, the box keeps its old height for one draw, while the size callback measures the new one, then glides to it. A row's panel, its body and buttons, fades in as it opens and out as it closes. A closing row keeps its last panel drawn without clicks while it fades, and its box holds the panel's height until the fade ends, then leaves it out. A panel that shows nothing yet holds nothing. A panel that waits for its space shows nothing from its first draw, so a panel the row's former form drew does not fade out under the new form as it fades in. A row's title sits in its own box, at its closed form's height unless the row is open, and the lines under the title glide with that box. The closed form is the title on up to two lines, cut at a word, or one line on a settled row. A title longer than its closed form is drawn in both forms while its row opens or closes, its closed form over its whole form, since they wrap at different places. As a row opens, its title box glides to the whole title's height while the title keeps its closed form. Once the box comes to rest (`titleSwapMs`), the title fades to its wrapped form over `ENTER_FADE_MS`, as the panel fades in (`titleFades`). As a row closes or collapses, the title fades to its closed form over `LEAVE_FADE_MS` from the draw after the close starts, as the panel fades out, while the box holds the whole title's height. The box then glides to the closed form's height. In both fades the form the title takes fades in over the whole fade, and the form it leaves fades out over the second half, so the words both forms share never dim. A close whose box holds for less than `LEAVE_FADE_MS`, or does not hold, as one before the panel showed, takes the closed form at once. The trunk beside a wrapped title's lines, and beside the lines between the title and the panel, fades with the panel, so the tree comes and goes whole. A line between the title and the panel that comes or goes fades only its words, and its trunk stays. Every tree line fades with what it joins, as in the Codex tab. A column between two blocks that stay never fades, so the tree stays whole past a block that comes or goes. A column or arm that ends at a block that enters or leaves fades with that block: out while its box holds, and in once its space is nearly open. So no line shows while what it joins is hidden, and none fades while the rows it joins slide. A row that settles, or comes back through Undo, fades the lines under its title out over `LEAVE_FADE_MS` from the draw after the change, while its box holds its height, with its age when the two forms' differ, and its mark when one form has none. A mark that turns into another takes its new form when the new lines come, with no fade, as the Codex tab's does, so its column never shows empty. Its title fades between its one line and its two-line cut as when a row closes or opens, with the cut as the taller form. Then it draws its new form's lines at no opacity, its box glides to the new height, and the lines fade in over `ENTER_FADE_MS`. So no line pops in either direction, and the box never cuts a line that shows. A row the selection opens while its own or another row's lines fade out waits for them, as it waits for a closing panel. A change back to the form the row left, while its lines still fade out, fades them back in at once, its panel included. A change to a third form lets them finish fading out. A move that turns back starts from where the box is. The Codex tab moves a title the same way, with the closed row's clamped title as its short form, since that is how each look draws a closed title. Herdr sets an opening title's swap one draw after the move, once it has measured the wrapped height. When the selection moves, the closing row's panel fades first. Then the closing and the opening row's heights start to move together, so the rows below them move once. Every block, a heading or a fold as much as a row, that leaves fades out over `LEAVE_FADE_MS` while its box holds its height, then springs shut. A block that enters springs its box open, and its content fades in over `ENTER_FADE_MS` once the box stays within half of `SLIDE_PX` of open (`enterDelayMs`), as in the Codex tab. While a leaving block holds its space, nothing that a publish moves grows: a block that enters waits to open, a row the publish selects keeps its last form, and a row whose close starts with or during the hold keeps its height while its panel fades. So the closing and the opening row start to move together, as the leaving space starts to close. A Status line whose depth or connectors change keeps its old place in the tree during that hold (`holdsShape`), so the lines under a removed goal move once it has faded. Outside the hold, as when a field the person opened takes the place of the lines that leave, such a line takes its new form at once, since its old connectors would join lines that already hid. While a closing row's panel fades, a block that enters and a row that opens wait too, the person's own moves included, so the list does not grow before it shrinks. A row opened again while it still closes grows at once, and only its own panel's hold ends. Its panel fades back in at once, from the opacity it has. If its panel space is not at rest at full height, as when its box had begun to shrink or still regrows from an earlier turn-back, the panel fades to 0.1 opacity, `FAINT_OPACITY`, as fast as an opening panel's fade passes it, and holds there until its space is nearly open, then to full over `ENTER_FADE_MS` (`turnBackFades`), as in the Codex tab. Herdr draws again when each step starts. So the regrowing box cuts the panel's lines only while they are under 0.2 opacity, `CUT_OPACITY`. A row closed again before its panel's last step starts holds nothing. Its panel steps at once to 0.1 opacity, `FAINT_OPACITY`, if it shows more, then fades out over `LEAVE_FADE_MS` while its space shuts from where it is (`faintCloseFades`), as in the Codex tab. So the shutting edge cuts it only while it is under `CUT_OPACITY`. A row that opens fades its panel in once its space is nearly open, the same for the person's own key, click or press as for a publish. A status under a row's buttons, such as "Sending…", that the person's press brings shows faint in the press's draw, at `FAINT_OPACITY`, and fades to full once the row's box is nearly open, so the growing box cuts it only while it is that faint. A status that changes without the person's press is hidden until the box is nearly open, then fades in. Either fades out while the box holds its height, and goes at once when a press takes it away. A status that comes back while it fades out keeps its space and fades back in from the opacity it shows. When the person's press brings it back, it shows faint at once instead, as a status a press brings does. A status whose kind changes, as "Explaining…" that turns into "Queued: Explain", "Sending…" that turns into a longer "Not sent" or "Not applied", or a press under way, as "Refreshing PR #31…", that turns into how it ended, fades out while its space holds. The new status then comes in that space, hidden for a draw while its height is measured, and fades in. A status still coming in, which its growing box may cut, comes again from that draw instead, since its box grows again. A status that shows while the row's box grows above it, as when Explain's reply adds lines or a press opens the Reply field, steps to `FAINT_OPACITY` and fades back in once the row's box is nearly open (`pushUnder`), so the box cuts it only while it is that faint. When a row changes form, as one that comes back through Undo, the status of the form it leaves fades out from the opacity it shows, while the row's box holds its height. So a status still fading in, as Undo's "Sending…", never shows whole. The Codex tab shows "Sending…" at once in the title line's age slot, and its press line shows faint at once and fades to full once its space is nearly open, as here. A row that closes or settles takes that status away with its panel. A status that comes above the buttons of a row that held no line there opens a box of its own from no height, and the row's box grows with it, so the buttons glide down. So does a status that comes after the title of a row that drew no buttons and no line there, as "Not applied" on a row folded while its press was out. It shows faint in its first draw, so a press shows at once, then fades in once its box is nearly open. A status above the buttons or after the title that turns into one of another kind, as a status line that turns into "Not applied", fades out while its space holds. The new status then comes in that space, hidden for a draw while it is measured, its box gliding from the old line's height. A status after the title that goes fades out the same way, then its space closes. In a draw that follows the person's input, the new status comes at once, so a press shows. The lines an open row adds to its body, such as the options "All N options" adds or the context a question recorded again adds above its answers, open a box of their own the same way, and the buttons under them glide down. They show faint until they have measured, then fade in once their box is nearly open. A row that collapses keeps its open form's lines above its panel while the panel fades and its box holds its height. A kept line its closed form drops fades out with the panel. Then a line its closed form adds there, such as "Sending…", fades in over `ENTER_FADE_MS` from the next draw, so the panel never moves down while it shows. When the person's press closes the row and its closed form adds such a line, as Address's "Addressing…", the panel steps to `FAINT_OPACITY` in the press's draw and fades out while its box holds its height. The line shows at `FAINT_OPACITY` over the top of the panel, taking no space, then fades to full in its own place once the panel is left out. So the press shows in its first draw and nothing moves. A status that comes or goes there once the panel has faded moves in a box of its own, as on a row at rest. A PR's status line, such as "Ready to merge", sits in a box of its own whose height springs, and so does each line under it: the PR's error, its buttons and its press result. The PR's box is the sum of its title's height and those boxes', so each line moves with the edge of the box above it. Each line comes and goes as a status under a row's buttons does, an error or press result the person's press brings included. In a narrow pane the PR's number leads that line, so the line never comes or goes. How blocks move after the person's own key, click or press is in [the Herdr look's motion after input](differences-herdr-input-motion.md). A leave turned back fades back in from where it is, or once its box is nearly open if the box had begun to shut or had not yet opened. A turned-back block whose box had begun to shut waits to open while another leaving block holds its space, as a block that enters does, and so does the Codex tab's. A leaving block whose box is shut holds no space. In a draw that starts a move, a group's title and the tab bar's counts keep their last form, so they change in the draw that sets the rows moving. A group title's count that goes, as when its last question closes, fades out over `LEAVE_FADE_MS` from the next draw. A tab's count that goes fades out the same way while its box holds its width, then the box springs shut, so the tabs after it glide. A count that comes springs its box open from no width, then fades in once the box is nearly open (`TabCounts`), as in the Codex tab. A leaving block stays drawn until its spring comes to rest. A row selected as it enters keeps gliding to its open height. A fold's blocks enter as it opens and leave as it shuts. A leaving block stays after the nearest block above it that is still drawn. A block that stays on the page and moves to another place leaves a copy of itself in its old place, which leaves as any block does, and enters at its new place, so the rows between the two places glide instead of jumping. A block that moves again while it still enters does the same: its copy leaves from where it is drawn, so no box jumps to the new place. The space above each block is a box of its own whose height springs. It is measured from the last block above that is not shut, so a block that enters starts with no space and a block that leaves ends with none. A move that turns back goes from where it is. The tab bar, the banners and the trimmed-session note above the list glide the list down or up as they change height: a tab bar that wraps to another line in a narrow pane, or a banner that comes or goes. Their words appear and go with the height, without a fade. A newly shown tab, Help page or session draws at rest. It keeps taking its heights at once while each draw brings new measures, for up to 100 ms, as when a question it shows for the first time measures its answers too wide to sit side by side, so the row comes to rest at its measured height instead of springing to it under its buttons. A box whose height changed with its width, as when the pane is resized, takes its new height at once, even mid-move. A leaving block's buttons, text field and fold stay drawn but take no clicks. A block that takes another block's id, as a dismissed PR's block after Undo, is that block in another form, so it glides between the two forms. A block that keeps its id and changes kind, as a PR's block that a fetch finds closed, fades its old form out over `LEAVE_FADE_MS` while its box holds, and that form takes no clicks. It then draws its new form hidden until it measures, and fades it in once its box is nearly at the new height (`BlockSwap`). A Status line does the same with only the parts that change: the question it waits on, the lines under it, its buttons, which take no clicks while they fade out, and a mark that comes or goes. A mark that turns into another takes its new form when the new parts come, with no fade. A part that starts to change while the others fade out fades out too, and the new parts wait for it. A part it gains where it had none comes hidden at once, then fades in. A note or a line whose text changes, as a PR's order line when the agent orders the PRs again, fades whole the same way. Its new form measures under a key of its own, since Herdr reports a box's new height a draw late, and its box glides from the old height. In a draw that follows the person's input, as a press's Dismiss or Undo, the change shows at once, so a press shows what it did. A Status line takes its new parts at once. A block that changes kind, a note or a line skips its fade out and shows its new form at `FAINT_OPACITY`, then fades to full once its box is nearly at the new height, so the box cuts it only while it is that faint. Motion always runs. | The Herdr app draws natively and has springs of its own, and a spring eases in and out where a fixed ease-out reads as stiff. It does not overshoot, since a box clips its content and an overshoot would show blank space under its last line. Opacity keeps the shared fades, since an opacity spring could overshoot past full. A spring has no fixed length, so a block stays drawn until its spring has come to rest, which `core/view/springs.ts` computes from the spring's numbers. gpui-kit animates only a later change of its target, and only between heights the mod sets, so every box draws at a height measured with the size callback, never at `auto` during a move. A fold's shut blocks are not drawn, so they are measured in the draw that opens the fold, not before: drawing them hidden would put them in the keys and the accessibility tree. A tab or Help page is a new page with nothing in common with the last one, so it draws at once, as the Codex tab draws a new tab. A width change rewraps every block at once, and a glide would make the text lag the pane. The banners are not list blocks, so they do not keep their last words while they go. A box's spring belongs to its element id, so a block entering at its new place under the boxes it had in its old place would start from the height they were drawn at, and its boxes take new ids. A fade belongs to its element id in the same way. An element with no id is known by its place in the tree, which shifts as blocks enter above it, and its fade then does not run. So each block's drawing and each tree line carries an id of its own. gpui-kit starts a fade whose target changes during its delay from where the fade would be with no delay, so a fade taken back before it starts can show near full opacity while a box cuts it. So Herdr hands gpui-kit no fade delay. A fade that waits, as for a box to be nearly open, keeps its former target until the wait ends, and Herdr draws again then to start it (`HeldFades`). A title keeps one weight in every state, in both looks, since a weight change reflows its words while it moves. Padding cannot glide, so every distance that can change is the height of a box whose spring is declared in every draw: each space, each block's content and each row's title. A move that starts while another glides then starts where every box is drawn. A move's first draw only sets where its boxes start, so a title or count that took its new form there would change a frame before the rows move. The Codex tab draws a group's empty note inside the element that ends the group, so that element's height move waits for a leave, while Herdr draws the note as a block of its own that enters and waits as any entering block does. A row a publish selects during a leave keeps its last form in Herdr, since its title box wraps in the open form's first draw and its panel would draw into a box held shut. A row the person selects while a closing panel fades keeps its closed form in Herdr, drawn selected, for the same reason. The Codex tab draws that row selected at once and holds its height with a delayed glide, its panel hidden until the space is nearly open.
