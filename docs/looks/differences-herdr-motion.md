# Differences on purpose: the Herdr look's motion

Part of [the look map](../looks.md#differences-on-purpose).

This part of the Herdr look's table covers how blocks move. Like [the rest of the table](differences-herdr.md), it compares the Herdr look with the Codex tab.

| Topic | Herdr look | Why | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Motion | Every change of a block's height moves on a spring with no overshoot, `HEIGHT_SPRING` in `core/view/springs.ts`, and every fade runs over the shared lengths: a row opening or closing, a status line coming or going, buttons, the reply field, a PR's order lines. Each block's content sits in a box at its measured height. When the content changes, the box keeps its old height for one draw, while the size callback measures the new one, then glides to it. A row's panel, its body and buttons, fades in as it opens and out as it closes. A closing row keeps its last panel drawn without clicks while it fades, and its box holds the panel's height until the fade ends, then leaves it out. A panel that shows nothing yet holds nothing. A row's title sits in its own box, one line high unless the row is open, and the lines under the title glide with that box. A title that wraps when open is drawn in both forms while its row opens or closes, its one-line form over its wrapped form, since they wrap at different places. As a row opens, its title box glides to the wrapped height while the title keeps its one-line form. Once the box comes to rest (`titleSwapMs`), the title fades to its wrapped form over `ENTER_FADE_MS`, as the panel fades in (`titleFades`). As a row closes or collapses, the title fades to its one-line form over `LEAVE_FADE_MS` from the draw after the close starts, as the panel fades out, while the box holds the wrapped height. The box then glides to one line. In both fades the form the title takes fades in over the whole fade, and the form it leaves fades out over the second half, so the words both forms share never dim. A close whose box holds for less than `LEAVE_FADE_MS`, or does not hold, as one before the panel showed, takes the one-line form at once. The trunk beside a wrapped title's lines fades with the panel, so the tree comes and goes whole. Every tree line fades with what it joins, as in the Codex tab. A column between two blocks that stay never fades, so the tree stays whole past a block that comes or goes. A column or arm that ends at a block that enters or leaves fades with that block: out while its box holds, and in once its space is nearly open. So no line shows while what it joins is hidden, and none fades while the rows it joins slide. A row that settles takes its one-line title, its settled line and Undo at once, in place of its open form, and its box glides to the settled height under them. So the box never cuts a line of the title that shows. A move that turns back starts from where the box is. The Codex tab moves a title the same way, with the closed row's clamped title as its short form, since that is how each look draws a closed title. Herdr sets an opening title's swap one draw after the move, once it has measured the wrapped height. When the selection moves, the closing row's panel fades first. Then the closing and the opening row's heights start to move together, so the rows below them move once. Every block, a heading or a fold as much as a row, that leaves fades out over `LEAVE_FADE_MS` while its box holds its height, then springs shut. A block that enters springs its box open, and its content fades in over `ENTER_FADE_MS` once the box stays within half of `SLIDE_PX` of open (`enterDelayMs`), as in the Codex tab. While a leaving block holds its space, nothing that a publish moves grows: a block that enters waits to open, a row the publish selects keeps its last form, and a row whose close starts with or during the hold keeps its height while its panel fades. So the closing and the opening row start to move together, as the leaving space starts to close. While a closing row's panel fades, a block that enters and a row that opens wait too, the person's own moves included, so the list does not grow before it shrinks. A row opened again while it still closes grows at once, and only its own panel's hold ends. Its panel fades back in at once, from the opacity it has. If its panel space is not at rest at full height, as when its box had begun to shrink or still regrows from an earlier turn-back, the panel fades to 0.5 opacity until its space is nearly open, then to full over `ENTER_FADE_MS` (`turnBackFades`), as in the Codex tab. Herdr draws again when the second step starts. So the regrowing box cuts the panel's lines only while they are under 0.5 opacity, the bound the frame harness checks for a turned-back panel, where it checks 0.2 for all other text. A row closed again before its panel's last step starts holds nothing. Its panel steps at once to 0.2 opacity if it shows more, then fades out over `LEAVE_FADE_MS` while its space shuts from where it is (`faintCloseFades`), as in the Codex tab. So the shutting edge cuts it only at 0.2 opacity or under. A row that opens fades its panel in once its space is nearly open, the same for the person's own key, click or press as for a publish. A status under a row's buttons, such as "Sending…", shows in the draw of the press that brings it and goes at once when a press takes it away. A status that changes without the person's press fades in once the row's box is nearly open, and fades out while the box holds its height. The Codex tab shows "Sending…" at once in the title line's age slot, and fades its press line in once its space is nearly open. A row that closes or settles takes that status away with its panel. The person's own moves do not wait for a leave. In a draw that follows a key, click or press, a row the selection opens waits only for the closing panel's fade, about 120 to 136 ms. A press's text field shows as soon as its box opens. Its buttons fade in once the box is nearly open, so the box's edge never cuts them. The lines it takes the place of, such as the goal's text, hide at once and shut without holding anything. A field closed while its box is still short of open, by more than an entering block waits for before its content fades in, hides at once and its box springs shut from where it is, while the lines it took the place of grow back from where they are. A field closed once its box is nearly open leaves as any block does. So the page never passes its closed height. A leave turned back fades back in from where it is, or once its box is nearly open if the box had begun to shut or had not yet opened. A turned-back block whose box had begun to shut waits to open while another leaving block holds its space, as a block that enters does, and so does the Codex tab's. A leaving block whose box is shut holds no space. In a draw that starts a move, a group's title and the tab bar's counts keep their last form, so they change in the draw that sets the rows moving. A leaving block stays drawn until its spring comes to rest. A row selected as it enters keeps gliding to its open height. A fold's blocks enter as it opens and leave as it shuts. A leaving block stays after the nearest block above it that is still drawn. A block that stays on the page and moves to another place leaves a copy of itself in its old place, which leaves as any block does, and enters at its new place, so the rows between the two places glide instead of jumping. The space above each block is a box of its own whose height springs. It is measured from the last block above that is not shut, so a block that enters starts with no space and a block that leaves ends with none. A move that turns back goes from where it is. The tab bar, the banners and the trimmed-session note above the list glide the list down or up as they change height: a tab bar that wraps to another line in a narrow pane, or a banner that comes or goes. Their words appear and go with the height, without a fade. A newly shown tab, Help page or session draws at rest. A box whose height changed with its width, as when the pane is resized, takes its new height at once, even mid-move. A leaving block's buttons, text field and fold stay drawn but take no clicks. A block that takes another block's id, as a dismissed PR's block after Undo, is that block in another form, so it glides between the two forms. Motion always runs. | The Herdr app draws natively and has springs of its own, and a spring eases in and out where a fixed ease-out reads as stiff. It does not overshoot, since a box clips its content and an overshoot would show blank space under its last line. Opacity keeps the shared fades, since an opacity spring could overshoot past full. A spring has no fixed length, so a block stays drawn until its spring has come to rest, which `core/view/springs.ts` computes from the spring's numbers. gpui-kit animates only a later change of its target, and only between heights the mod sets, so every box draws at a height measured with the size callback, never at `auto` during a move. A fold's shut blocks are not drawn, so they are measured in the draw that opens the fold, not before: drawing them hidden would put them in the keys and the accessibility tree. A tab or Help page is a new page with nothing in common with the last one, so it draws at once, as the Codex tab draws a new tab. A width change rewraps every block at once, and a glide would make the text lag the pane. The banners are not list blocks, so they do not keep their last words while they go. A box's spring belongs to its element id, so a block entering at its new place under the boxes it had in its old place would start from the height they were drawn at, and its boxes take new ids. A fade belongs to its element id in the same way. An element with no id is known by its place in the tree, which shifts as blocks enter above it, and its fade then does not run. So each block's drawing and each tree line carries an id of its own. A title keeps one weight in every state, in both looks, since a weight change reflows its words while it moves. Padding cannot glide, so every distance that can change is the height of a box whose spring is declared in every draw: each space, each block's content and each row's title. A move that starts while another glides then starts where every box is drawn. A move's first draw only sets where its boxes start, so a title or count that took its new form there would change a frame before the rows move. The Codex tab draws a group's empty note inside the element that ends the group, so that element's height move waits for a leave, while Herdr draws the note as a block of its own that enters and waits as any entering block does. A row a publish selects during a leave keeps its last form in Herdr, since its title box wraps in the open form's first draw and its panel would draw into a box held shut. A row the person selects while a closing panel fades keeps its closed form in Herdr, drawn selected, for the same reason. The Codex tab draws that row selected at once and holds its height with a delayed glide, its panel hidden until the space is nearly open. The Herdr app tells a mod no reduced-motion setting. | `herdr/src/motion.ts:moveRows`, `herdr/src/under-line.ts:stepUnder`, `herdr/src/paint-space.ts:spacedEls`, `herdr/src/paint-strokes.ts:strokeFadesOf`, `herdr/src/paint-space.ts:fitEl`, `herdr/src/paint-blocks.ts:rowEl`, `herdr/src/heights.ts:Heights`, `core/view/springs.ts:HEIGHT_SPRING` |
