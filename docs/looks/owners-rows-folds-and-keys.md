| Settled rows: a row closed or pressed since the look began showing its tab stays in place with its result for `SETTLED_MS`, the time Undo lasts, then folds into its group's Closed fold. It folds sooner when the person switches tab or closes the inbox, or sends a message they typed once its Undo has ended. A message a press sent does not fold it. A row stays past that time while its message is held for Undo or on its way to the agent (`core/view/timing.ts:isSendOnItsWay`), so a redraw at Undo end never folds it before its message leaves, and while a press that began before it came due is still under way on a row of its tab: a talk press waiting on its reply, a Compare press until the agent reports its comparison (the question's `reportedAt`) or `COMPARE_LIMIT_MS` passes, a message sending, or an open or copy running (`core/view/feedback.ts:pressUnderWaySince`, `core/view/timing.ts:isPressOutSince`). A refused or unconfirmed message does not hold it, and its Closed line shows what happened. It also waits while the person has been using the list since before it came due, until `FOLD_QUIET_MS` after their last press, key, scroll or pointer move. A hold keeps only a row that has not folded yet: a press or input that begins after the row came due leaves it folded, and a message typed later never moves its due time back. Once folded, a row stays folded until the look shows its tab again. A PR dismissed during the showing settles the same way. | `core/view/timing.ts:isSettledInPlace`, `core/view/timing.ts:FOLD_QUIET_MS`, `core/view/timing.ts:withListInput`, `core/view/timing.ts:nextFoldAt`, `core/view/timing.ts:isShownSince`, `core/view/timing.ts:isDrawnInPlace`, `core/view/view.ts:inboxView`, `core/view/rows.ts:closedRowState`, `core/view/pr-views.ts:drawnPrs`, `core/runtime/session-turns.ts:messageTyped`, `core/view/timing.ts:SETTLED_MS` | All four looks.
