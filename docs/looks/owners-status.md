# Owners: the Status page

Part of [the look map](../looks.md#who-owns-each-rule).

| Rule | Owner | Read by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The order of Status rows: ◆, ● and ◐, ◌, ○, then ✓ and ✗ together, each group newest change first, whether or not the person has seen them. In each part of the list, the "○ N more" fold and Other work's counts follow the open rows, the named folds follow the closed rows, and the done fold comes last. While a goal shows milestones, the rows of work that serves a milestone draw under it, in that order, and every other row draws under Other work, in that order too. Running work with no shown subject draws first under its subject's milestone, else first under Other work. While a goal shows no milestones, the rows after the goal are the work not linked to a milestone yet, then Other work. | `hooks/status-page.ts:STATUS_PLACES`, `hooks/status-page.ts:statusPage`. `MilestoneRow.rows` holds a milestone's rows. `hooks/status-folds.ts:statusListRows` gives every row in draw order, for readers such as `hooks/timing.ts:hasRunningMark` and the evals. | All four looks draw each milestone's `rows` under it, then `StatusPage.rows`, in order. |
| The "N more" fold: which open subjects the Status draws, and which fold into one row | `hooks/status-page.ts:statusPage` draws at most `hooks/status-limits.ts:OPEN_SUBJECTS` open subjects, the number the Status call is told to keep. It folds the rest into "○ N more", taking the rows it would draw last: queued ones first, then those that wait on others, then working ones. Three kinds of subject never fold, so more can show: one an open ask holds up, one that changed since the last look, and one with a child row under it. A page the person has never looked at folds nothing. The fold's mark is always ○. While a goal is set, the fold is chosen across all the work the goal needs. A milestone's folded open subjects fold into its own "○ N more" row after its open rows, and its seen finished ones into its own "N done" row after all its rows, both one step in. Work under a milestone that closed since the last look follows the named fold's rule. | All four looks draw the `fold` row's mark, tone and text. |
| The done fold (`status-done-fold`): seen finished work folded into "N done", or "N more done" while finished rows still show above it. Other work's `counts` row folds the same way | `hooks/status-page.ts:statusPage` builds a `done-fold` row for the list after the goal, for each milestone and for Other work, with the finished rows it holds in `rows`, one step in, in the page's order. It has no mark. The list after the goal's fold, or Other work's while the goal shows milestones, also holds each finished delegate that no subject holds once the person has seen it (`hooks/status-children.ts:isChildSettled`: it ended by `lastLookAt`, within `CHILD_KEPT_MS`), as a `child` row after the subjects' rows. Until a look ends it draws as a loose row, so a delegate that finishes while the page is in view stays where it is. A finished delegate that a subject holds leaves with the look, as its subject's own row stands for it. `hooks/status-folds.ts:unfoldedRows` puts those rows after each fold the person unfolded. `hooks/status-keys.ts:statusKeyPlan` gives it one key, `toggle-fold`, so j and k reach it in draw order. Each starts folded. | All four looks draw it as their Closed fold: the count, muted, where a row's text starts, then the chevron, › folded and v unfolded, as every fold line draws it. A press unfolds it, and another folds it. The terminal draws the Closed fold's Button, and desktop its Client (`hooks/pane-row-parts.tsx:foldRow`). The mod saves the unfolded folds' ids in `statusUnfolded`, Codex in the tab's kept state, and omp in `PanelUi.statusUnfolded`, as each keeps its Closed folds. |
| The named fold (`status-named-fold`): work that closed since the last look, past the rows drawn for it, named, as "✓ Done: Sync merge, Search speed, +3 more" and "✗ Dropped: Flick option" | `hooks/status-page.ts:statusPage` draws at most `NEW_CLOSED_ROWS` rows of work that closed since the last look, in each part of the list: the list after the goal, each milestone, and Other work. It names the rest in a `fold` row for each closed status, done first, with the words in `NAMED_FOLD_TEXT`. Each fold shows at most `NAMED_FOLD_NAMES` names, the newest first, and counts the rest as "+N more", so the line wraps to at most `STATUS_TEXT_LINES` lines at the narrowest terminal dock and omp never cuts it. `hooks/status-page-parts.ts:partRows` puts these folds after the part's closed rows. They hold no rows and take no press. Each fold carries in `subjectIds` the work it stands for, the named work and the work its "+N more" counts, as `IS_FOLD_MORE_SEEN` sets. `hooks/status-page.ts:seenSubjectIds` gives the work a row stands for, so a look that shows the fold counts that work as seen. | All four looks draw the `fold` row's mark, tone and text. Each look records the fold as showing the work in its `subjectIds`, as it records a subject row as showing its subject. |
| The parent fold (`status-parent-fold`): a subject or milestone with rows under it folds them behind its name, and opens again on its own when something under it starts waiting on the person | `hooks/status-page.ts:statusPage` sets `foldCount` on each subject row and milestone: the rows one step under it, counting what its done folds hold. A subject counts its delegates, and a milestone its subjects and the running work linked to it. It is 0 when there are none, or on a done milestone folded to its name. The person's folds are saved in `Session.collapsed`, as a collapsed row is, by the parent's id, through each host's row-collapse press. `hooks/status-folds.ts:waitingUnderParents` says what waits on the person under each parent: under a subject, its running delegates Herdr shows blocked, and under a milestone, also each open ask that holds up a subject serving it. `hooks/collapse.ts:withCollapse` saves those ids with the fold, and `collapsedRows` opens the fold once an id that was not there waits. Every write saves the collapses through `hooks/collapse.ts:settledCollapses`, which drops a fold that opened this way, so it stays open, and keeps only the saved ids still waiting, so one that waits again opens the fold. A parent left with nothing under it loses its fold. `hooks/status-folds.ts:unfoldedRows`, `milestoneRows` and `drawnStatusRows` leave out the rows under a folded parent, and `parentChevron` gives what follows its name, v while open and › and its `foldCount` while folded. `hooks/status-keys.ts:statusKeyRows` gives the parent's target a `toggle-collapse` key on `f`, which no look draws as a button. | All five looks draw the parent's name line as its fold, with the chevron after the name and its stall word, muted, and draw no tree line down from a folded parent. A press on the name folds it and another opens it. The terminal draws the chevron as a dim Button, and j, k and `f` reach and fold the parent. Desktop draws the name line as a `Client` that takes the click across the line. Codex draws the name line as a button that also takes Enter and Space while focused, with no hotkey (see Differences on purpose), and the rows leave and enter as a milestone's rows do. omp takes j, k and `f`. Herdr takes a click across the name line, mark included, with no key, since its Status page has no row selection, and the rows glide on `HEIGHT_SPRING` as a done fold's do. Each look leaves the hidden rows out of what it reports as shown. |
| Status row lines and tree: how many lines each text on a row wraps to before a look cuts it with an ellipsis, how far in each row sits, which rows hang under a subject, and which is last | `hooks/status-page.ts:STATUS_TEXT_LINES` is the line limit. `hooks/status-page.ts:statusPage` sets `depth` on every row: 0 after the goal, 1 for a subject under a milestone, and one more for the child rows under a subject. It sets `branch` on child and more rows, and `hasChildren` on subject rows. A child row with no `branch` is linked to no subject the list shows, and sits at the top. `hooks/status-path.ts:workJoints` says where each of a milestone's rows meets the milestones' mark column: a `tee` or `elbow` arm to the row's mark, and what runs past it, the nesting connector or the path's line. | All four looks. Each wraps in its own units, and cuts with `hooks/text.ts:cutForEllipsis`, except Codex: the terminal through `hooks/terminal-text.ts:terminalSpanLines`, desktop through `hooks/terminal-text.ts:desktopSpanLines`, Codex with `-webkit-line-clamp`, and omp through `omp/src/panel-page.ts:pageLine`. Each draws the tree line its own way: see "Tree lines on the Status" in Differences on purpose. |
| Where an Status row stands, its stall word, what it waits on, its merged line, and a child named like its subject | `hooks/status-page.ts:statusPage` sets `stall` on subject rows, as text in the muted tone. A subject that an open ask holds up sets `waitsOn` to the words of the oldest such ask (`hooks/status-page-parts.ts:askWords`), on one line in the needs-you tone, with no prefix, and has no stall word. The line wraps and clips to `STATUS_TEXT_LINES` as where it stands does, and sits between the name and where it stands. A subject that waits on someone else shows no word. `hooks/status-page-parts.ts:partRows` draws no row for the only child of an open subject when `isNamedLikeSubject` finds its name equal to the subject's, ignoring case, spaces, hyphens and underscores. The subject's row carries that child's state (`carriedByChild`) while the subject is open: a moving ● while the child works, ◆ while it is blocked, and ✓ once it finished, unless an open ask holds the subject up, which keeps its own mark and words. Such a subject has `hasChildren` false, so no tree line runs from it. An open subject unchanged for `IDLE_AFTER_MS` (a day), with no child of it running and no ask holding it, reads how long it has been idle in whole days, as "idle 2d", in the muted tone (`hooks/status-page-parts.ts:subjectStall`). A child and a milestone have no stall word: the ◆ mark in the needs-you tone shows a blocked delegate or a held milestone, and a finished delegate's ✓ mark says it finished. How long a subject has been idle is the only time a row shows. `hooks/status-page.ts:mergedLine` writes "merged in:" and the names of the subjects dropped into a subject, cut to one line at a phrase by `hooks/text.ts:cutAtPhrase`. | All four looks. Each draws a row's name, then `STALL_GAP` and the muted stall word, then the `waitsOn` line in the needs-you tone (the terminal and desktop in `hooks/pane-status.tsx`, Codex in `codex/src/tab-status-line.tsx`, omp in `omp/src/panel-page.ts:pageLine`, Herdr in `herdr/src/paint-blocks.ts`). A wrap never splits the name's last word, the gap and the stall word, so the word always shares a line with the name. When the row runs past its lines, `hooks/status-page.ts:nameBeforeStall` cuts the name at a word, and the stall word stays whole. A subject then draws where it stands, muted, then the merged line, then the was line. Each look passes `mergedLine` its own width test. Codex measures the merged line in a canvas, and counts the name line's lines as the browser lays them out. |
| Rules on the Status page: which parts a rule sets apart | Sections get rules and rows do not. No row draws a rule of its own, and nothing draws a rule between the goal and the list after it. While the goal shows milestones, that list starts with Other work's labelled rule. | Every look. Desktop and Codex hide the line they draw between other sections there: see "The space after the goal" in Differences on purpose. |
| Running marks: which Status rows run, which result marks run, and how fast a mark moves | `hooks/status-page.ts:statusPage` sets `StatusRow.isRunning` on subject and child rows, and a child row's `mark` and `tone`: ◆ in the needs-you tone for a delegate that waits, and ✓ in the done tone for one that finished. `ResultMark.isRunning` says a row's result mark runs. `hooks/timing.ts:hasRunningMark`, `hooks/arrivals.ts:isRowTabRunning` and `hooks/pr-rows.ts:isPrTabRunning` say whether a tab draws one, and `hooks/session-view.ts:showsRunningMark` picks the one for the shown tab. `PrRowMark.isRunning` says a PR thread's mark runs. `hooks/timing.ts:runningMark`, `hooks/timing.ts:RUNNING_FRAME_MS`, `hooks/timing.ts:RUNNING_PULSE_MS` | All four looks read `isRunning`. The terminal and omp start their timer from `showsRunningMark`: `hasRunningMark` on the Status, `isRowTabRunning` on the Asks and Finds tabs, `isPrTabRunning` on the PRs tab, and always while Help shows, since the legend's first mark runs. They draw `runningMark` each `RUNNING_FRAME_MS`. Desktop and Codex fade the mark once each `RUNNING_PULSE_MS`. A Talk press, such as Explaining…, reads its label and runs no mark. |
| Asks that hold up a subject on the Status | `hooks/status-ops.ts:applyOps` saves `Status.askSubjects` from the Status call's LINK lines that name an open item. `hooks/status-page.ts:statusPage` draws ◆ in the `needsYou` tone on an open subject while an open item links to it, in place of its status's mark. That subject never folds into "N more", and its mark does not run. | All four looks draw the row's `mark` and `tone`. |
| Buttons on an Status row: View question or View task, on a subject an open ask holds up. It opens Asks with the oldest of those asks selected, takes its label from that ask's kind, and sends nothing. j and k move between the rows that have buttons, in draw order, so a milestone's rows follow it, and only the selected row's keys work. With nothing selected, the first row with a View button is selected, else the first with any button other than a done fold, else the fold. | `hooks/status-keys.ts:statusKeyPlan`, `hooks/status-keys.ts:statusKeyRows`, `hooks/status-keys.ts:statusSelected`. `hooks/status-keys.ts:VIEW_ASK_LABELS`. `hooks/status-page.ts:statusPage` sets the subject row's `ask`. | All four looks draw the plan's buttons under the row's text, in the plan's order. The terminal draws them as a key row, marks the selected row the way a row tab does, and shows the key only on that row's button. Desktop draws native Buttons. Codex draws `.key` buttons that take clicks (see `status-view-ask` in Differences on purpose). omp draws the selected row's button focused, so Enter or its key presses it, and scrolls the page to the row j or k selects. omp holds the opened ask's buttons for `PRESS_GUARD_MS`, as the terminal and Codex do, so a second Enter does not press its recommended option. Each look runs the press itself: `hooks/register.tsx:showAsk`, `codex/src/tab-status-line.tsx:showAsk` and `omp/src/panel-keys.ts:pageKey`. |
| The "Holds up" line on an Asks row | `hooks/view.ts:inboxView` sets `RowView.holdsUpLine`, "Holds up" and the subject's name, on an open item that the Status call linked to an open subject or to an open milestone of a set goal. `hooks/help.ts:HELD_MARK` is the ◆ the Status marks the held subject with, and an open question's or task's row takes the same mark. | A collapsed row draws it as a muted line under the title, with no mark, in every look except the Herdr pane, which draws it only in the open row. The selected row draws it as the last branch of its tree (`asks-tree`, in "An open question's or task's tree"). The row Client draws the collapsed line as its `note` (`hooks/row-client.tsx`). The terminal draws it in `hooks/pane-row-lines.tsx:unselectedLine`, Codex as `.holds-up` in `codex/src/tab-list.tsx`, and omp under the title line in `omp/src/panel-draw.ts:drawLine`. |
