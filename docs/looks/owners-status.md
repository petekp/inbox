# Owners: the Status page

Part of [the look map](../looks.md#who-owns-each-rule).

| Rule | Owner | Read by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The order of Status rows: the rows that changed since the last look above the Seen line, and on each side of it ◆, ● and ◐, ◌, ○, then ✓ and ✗ together, each group newest change first. While a goal is set, the rows of work that serves a milestone draw under it, in that order, and the rows after the goal are the work not linked to a milestone yet, then Other work. | `hooks/status-page.ts:STATUS_PLACES`, `hooks/status-page.ts:statusPage`. `MilestoneRow.rows` holds a milestone's rows. `hooks/status-page.ts:statusListRows` gives every row in draw order, for a reader that needs all of them, such as `hooks/timing.ts:hasRunningMark` and the evals. | All four looks draw each milestone's `rows` under it, then `StatusPage.rows`, in order. |
| The "N more" fold: which open subjects the Status draws, and which fold into one row | `hooks/status-page.ts:statusPage` draws at most `hooks/status-limits.ts:OPEN_SUBJECTS` open subjects, the number the Status call is told to keep. It folds the rest into "○ N more", taking the rows it would draw last: queued ones first, then those that wait on others, then working ones. Three kinds of subject never fold, so more can show: one an open ask holds up, one that changed since the last look, and one with a child row under it. A page the person has never looked at folds nothing. The fold's mark is always ○. While a goal is set, the fold is chosen across the work under milestones and the work after the goal together. A milestone's folded open subjects and its seen finished ones fold into its own "○ N more" and "N done" rows, one step in, after its rows. Work under a milestone that closed since the last look follows the named fold's rule. | All four looks draw the `fold` row's mark, tone and text. |
| The done fold (`status-done-fold`): seen finished work folded into "N done", or "N more done" while finished rows still show above it | `hooks/status-page.ts:statusPage` builds a `done-fold` row for the list after the goal, for each milestone and for Other work, with the finished rows it holds in `rows`, one step in, in the page's order. It has no mark. `hooks/status-page-parts.ts:unfoldedRows` puts those rows after each fold the person unfolded. `hooks/status-keys.ts:statusKeyPlan` gives it one key, `toggle-fold`, so j and k reach it in draw order. Each starts folded. | All four looks draw it as their Closed fold: a chevron in the mark column, ▸ folded and ▾ unfolded, then the count, muted. A press unfolds it, and another folds it. The terminal draws the Closed fold's Button, and desktop its Client (`hooks/pane-row-parts.tsx:foldRow`). The mod saves the unfolded folds' ids in `statusUnfolded`, Codex in the tab's kept state, and omp in `PanelUi.statusUnfolded`, as each keeps its Closed folds. |
| The named fold (`status-named-fold`): work that closed since the last look, past the rows drawn for it, named, as "✓ Also done: Sync merge, Search speed, +3 more" and "✗ Also dropped: Flick option" | `hooks/status-page.ts:statusPage` draws at most `NEW_CLOSED_ROWS` rows of work that closed since the last look, in each part of the list: the list after the goal, each milestone, and Other work. It names the rest in a `fold` row for each closed status, done first, with the words in `NAMED_FOLD_TEXT`. Each fold shows at most `NAMED_FOLD_NAMES` names, the newest first, and counts the rest as "+N more", so the line wraps to at most `STATUS_TEXT_LINES` lines at the narrowest terminal dock and omp never cuts it. `hooks/status-page-parts.ts:partRows` puts these folds after the rows that changed, above the look line. They hold no rows and take no press. Each fold carries in `subjectIds` the work it stands for, the named work and the work its "+N more" counts, as `IS_FOLD_MORE_SEEN` sets. `hooks/status-page.ts:seenSubjectIds` gives the work a row stands for, so a look that shows the fold counts that work as seen. | All four looks draw the `fold` row's mark, tone and text. Each look records the fold as showing the work in its `subjectIds`, as it records a subject row as showing its subject. |
| Status row lines and tree: how many lines each text on a row wraps to before a look cuts it with an ellipsis, how far in each row sits, which rows hang under a subject, and which is last | `hooks/status-page.ts:STATUS_TEXT_LINES` is the line limit. `hooks/status-page.ts:statusPage` sets `depth` on every row: 0 after the goal, 1 for a subject under a milestone, and one more for the child rows under a subject. It sets `branch` on child and more rows, and `hasChildren` on subject rows. A child row with no `branch` is linked to no subject the list shows, and sits at the top. A milestone draws no tree line to the rows under it; their step in ties them to it. | All four looks. Each wraps in its own units, and cuts with `hooks/text.ts:cutForEllipsis`, except Codex: the terminal through `hooks/terminal-text.ts:terminalSpanLines`, desktop through `hooks/terminal-text.ts:desktopSpanLines`, Codex with `-webkit-line-clamp`, and omp through `omp/src/panel-page.ts:pageLine`. Each draws the tree line its own way: see "Tree lines on the Status" in Differences on purpose. |
| Where an Status row stands, its stall word, and its merged line | `hooks/status-page.ts:statusPage` sets `stall` on subject and child rows, as text and a tone. A subject that an open ask holds up reads "waiting" in the needs-you tone. A subject that waits on someone else shows no word. An open subject unchanged for `IDLE_AFTER_MS` (a day), with no child of it running and no ask holding it, reads how long it has been idle in whole days, as "idle 2d", in the muted tone (`hooks/status-page-parts.ts:subjectStall`). `hooks/status-page-parts.ts:childStall` writes a child's stall word from the state `hooks/children.ts:delegatesSettled` takes from Herdr: "waiting" in the needs-you tone for a blocked delegate, and "finished" in the done tone. Other children show none. How long a subject has been idle is the only time a row shows. A stall word's tone is needs-you, done or muted. `hooks/status-page.ts:mergedLine` writes "merged in:" and the names of the subjects dropped into a subject, cut to one line at a phrase by `hooks/text.ts:cutAtPhrase`. | All four looks. Each draws a row's name, then `STALL_GAP` and the stall word in its tone. A wrap never splits the name's last word, the gap and the stall word, so the word always shares a line with the name. When the row runs past its lines, `hooks/status-page.ts:nameBeforeStall` cuts the name at a word, and the stall word stays whole. A subject then draws where it stands, muted, then the merged line, then the was line. Each look passes `mergedLine` its own width test. Codex measures the merged line in a canvas, and counts the name line's lines as the browser lays them out. |
| Rules between Status rows: which rows a rule sets apart | `hooks/status-page-parts.ts:ruledRows` sets `StatusRow.isRuledAbove`. A subject and the rows under it are one item, and no rule sits beside the Seen line. The rows under a milestone draw no rules, as the milestones draw none, and neither do the rows a done fold holds. | All four looks. Each draws the rule its own way: see "Rules between Status rows" in Differences on purpose. |
| The Seen line's dashes, and the space above it and, unless it ends the list, below it | `hooks/status-page.ts:LOOK_DASH`, `hooks/layout.ts:LOOK_GAP_PT`. `hooks/status-page-parts.ts:ruledRows` sets `StatusRow.isLast`. Under a milestone, the Seen line draws one step in, and only between changed and seen rows, so busy milestones do not each end with one (`ListContext.isLookAtEnd`). | All four looks. Each draws them its own way: see "The Seen line" in Differences on purpose. |
| Running marks: which Status rows run, which result marks run, and how fast a mark moves | `hooks/status-page.ts:statusPage` sets `StatusRow.isRunning` on subject and child rows, and a child row's `mark` and `tone`: ◆ in the needs-you tone for a delegate that waits, and ✓ in the done tone for one that finished. `ResultMark.isRunning` says a row's result mark runs. `hooks/timing.ts:hasRunningMark`, `hooks/arrivals.ts:isRowTabRunning` and `hooks/pr-rows.ts:isPrTabRunning` say whether a tab draws one, and `hooks/session-view.ts:showsRunningMark` picks the one for the shown tab. `PrRowMark.isRunning` says a PR thread's mark runs. `hooks/timing.ts:runningMark`, `hooks/timing.ts:RUNNING_FRAME_MS`, `hooks/timing.ts:RUNNING_PULSE_MS` | All four looks read `isRunning`. The terminal and omp start their timer from `showsRunningMark`: `hasRunningMark` on the Status, `isRowTabRunning` on the Asks and Finds tabs, `isPrTabRunning` on the PRs tab, and always while Help shows, since the legend's first mark runs. They draw `runningMark` each `RUNNING_FRAME_MS`. Desktop and Codex fade the mark once each `RUNNING_PULSE_MS`. A Talk press, such as Explaining…, reads its label and runs no mark. |
| Asks that hold up a subject on the Status | `hooks/status-ops.ts:applyOps` saves `Status.askSubjects` from the Status call's LINK lines that name an open item. `hooks/status-page.ts:statusPage` draws ◆ in the `needsYou` tone on an open subject while an open item links to it, in place of its status's mark. That subject never folds into "N more", and its mark does not run. | All four looks draw the row's `mark` and `tone`. |
| Buttons on an Status row: View question or View task, on a subject an open ask holds up. It opens Asks with the oldest of those asks selected, takes its label from that ask's kind, and sends nothing. j and k move between the rows that have buttons, in draw order, so a milestone's rows follow it, and only the selected row's keys work. With nothing selected, the first row with a View button is selected, else the first with any button other than a done fold, else the fold. | `hooks/status-keys.ts:statusKeyPlan`, `hooks/status-keys.ts:statusKeyRows`, `hooks/status-keys.ts:statusSelected`. `hooks/status-keys.ts:VIEW_ASK_LABELS`. `hooks/status-page.ts:statusPage` sets the subject row's `ask`. | All four looks draw the plan's buttons under the row's text, in the plan's order. The terminal draws them as a key row, marks the selected row the way a row tab does, and shows the key only on that row's button. Desktop draws native Buttons. Codex draws `.key` buttons that take clicks (see `status-view-ask` in Differences on purpose). omp draws the selected row's button focused, so Enter or its key presses it, and scrolls the page to the row j or k selects. omp holds the opened ask's buttons for `PRESS_GUARD_MS`, as the terminal and Codex do, so a second Enter does not press its recommended option. Each look runs the press itself: `hooks/register.tsx:showAsk`, `codex/src/tab-status.tsx:showAsk` and `omp/src/panel-keys.ts:pageKey`. |
| The goal (`status-goal`): the goal the Status call set or the person typed, its milestones, and the Now line | `hooks/status-goal-page.ts:goalPage` builds `StatusPage.goal`, with its label, `GOAL_HEADING` or "Goal met", its count of done milestones and its Now line, `GoalBlock.now`. `hooks/status-page.ts:statusPage` fills each milestone's `rows`. `hooks/status-goal-ops.ts` applies GOAL, RETIRE, MILESTONE and SERVES lines, and `hooks/status-goal-gate.ts` says when code takes a GOAL or RETIRE line. A GOAL line sets the goal while none is set, once the goal is met, or over an inferred goal none of whose work under an open milestone is open, and only while an open subject sits outside the current goal. A refused line is saved as `goalRefused`, which only the next Status call reads. A typed goal stays until it is met. A goal that still waits for its milestones is not met, even when the milestones it carries from the goal before it are all done. A GOAL line restating a typed goal just set, from a call that read it, gives that goal its milestones. `settleGoal` closes each milestone whose linked work closed, and sets when the goal is met: every milestone left is done. A milestone is held, ◆ and "waiting", while an open ask links to it or to a subject that serves it. | All four looks draw the goal's section first on every page that is not empty, in the same shape with a goal or without one. Its heading row holds the goal's label, or `GOAL_HEADING` while no goal is set, muted, at the goal text's column, then a solid rule, then the goal's buttons at the right edge, a dim `STALL_GAP` (` · `) between each two. On desktop the dot is a dim Text between the native Buttons. When the buttons do not fit beside the title, the rule runs to the edge and they wrap to the next line, still at the right, never cut. Then the goal's text with its count muted after it, after `STALL_GAP`, where a wrap never splits the count from the text's last word, or `NO_GOAL_TEXT`, muted, while no goal is set. Then its was line and its Now line, muted, in place of the head, then the milestones. A blank line's space sits before each milestone, and between a milestone and the rows under it, so a milestone and its rows read as one group. The terminal and omp draw a blank line, and desktop and Codex `hooks/layout.ts:LOOK_GAP_PT`. A milestone draws like a subject row, with its name bold while it changed since the last look, and its rows under it one step in. While no goal is set, the head draws in its own section after the goal's, so it never reads as the goal. |
| The goal's buttons and its text field | `hooks/status-keys.ts:goalKeyPlan` gives New, after Undo while a removed goal can come back, then Edit while a goal is set or New once the goal is met, then Remove. The labels leave out "goal", since they sit on the Goal heading. Keys: `n` New, `e` Edit, `r` Remove, `u` Undo. `hooks/status-keys.ts:statusKeyRows` puts them first in the order j and k move, as the goal's section comes first. `hooks/goal-press.ts:goalPressed` runs Remove, its Undo and a typed goal through `hooks/pressed.ts:pressed`, on the id `goal`. None sends a message. | Every look draws the plan's buttons in order on the goal's heading row. Edit and New open the look's reply field under the draft id `goal`, with the plan's text in it. While the field is open, it takes the place of the goal's text, or of the line that says no goal is set, the buttons leave the heading, and no Status target is selected or has keys. On the empty page, New and its field draw under the empty state's text. Save, the field's submit, runs a `type` press on `goal`. Codex keeps the field open, its buttons dimmed, until the server confirms the goal, and shows why a goal press failed. `hooks/pane-status-goal.tsx`, `codex/src/tab-status.tsx` and the omp panel draw them. |
| Other work | `hooks/status-page.ts:statusPage` lists subjects whose SERVES is `none` under a `part` row, "Other work", while a goal is set. Seen open ones that hold nothing fold into one `counts` row, a count for each mark, and seen done ones into Other work's own done fold after it. A look line that would end the main list is left out, since the part's rule follows it. | All four looks draw the `part` row as its name and a rule, with the Seen line's space above it, and the `counts` row as each mark in its tone with its count, joined by " · ". The counts wrap onto more lines where they do not fit. |
| The "Holds up" line on an Asks row | `hooks/view.ts:inboxView` sets `RowView.holdsUpLine`, "Holds up" and the subject's name, on an open item that the Status call linked to an open subject or to an open milestone of a set goal. `hooks/help.ts:HELD_MARK` is the ◆ it starts with, the held subject's mark on the Status. | All four looks draw it on a next build only, since only a next build draws the Status. It is muted, under the title, in the collapsed and the selected row, after ◆ in the needs-you tone. The row Client draws the ◆ from its `noteMark`. The terminal draws it in `hooks/pane-row-lines.tsx:unselectedLine` and as the row's subtitle. Desktop draws it as the row Client's `note` (`hooks/row-client.tsx`) and as the subtitle. Codex draws it as `.holds-up` in `codex/src/tab-list.tsx`, and omp under the title line in `omp/src/panel-draw.ts:drawLine`. |
