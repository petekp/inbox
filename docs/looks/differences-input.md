# Differences on purpose: keys, clicks and replies

Part of [the look map](../looks.md#differences-on-purpose).

| Topic | Terminal | Desktop | Codex | Why | Where |

| Keys | Button hotkeys work. The key letter is drawn before the label. | Clicks only. Button hotkeys do nothing, and no letter is drawn. | A page `keydown` handler takes the letters. | Help (`help-page`) | A "?" tab at the right end of the tab row, opposite Status, Asks, Finds and PRs, opens it. While Help shows, the Help tab takes the selected tab's look and the four tabs read unselected, and a press on the Help tab or on any other tab leaves Help. It has no key letter: the engine binds only a digit or a lowercase letter, so `?` is refused, and no free letter reads as help. In the inline pane at 80 columns and wider, the tabs form a column at the left, and Help is its last entry, one blank line under Status, Asks, Finds and PRs, since a column has no right end. The Help tab's width counts in the tab row's fit, so tabs pad less and name themselves by symbols a little sooner. | A "?" tab at the right end of the first line of the tabs' Client, framed as the other tabs are. | Reply focus | The reply field, and the Status goal field, receive typing. | Leaving a reply field | Escape closes the field and keeps its draft. The pane stays and the prompt gets the keys. A second Escape closes the pane only at an idle, empty prompt. A click outside the pane also closes the field and keeps its draft. | Escape closes the field and keeps its draft, and the pane stays. A second Escape leaves the pane open, because the app does not close it on Escape. A click in the composer also closes the field and keeps its draft. Checked in the Claude app 3.6556.1. Expanding or collapsing the pane also closes an open field and keeps its draft, in the Claude app 4.4212.0. | Escape closes the field and keeps its draft. A click elsewhere leaves the field open. | The engine closes a pane opened with `closeOnEscape` on Escape, with the same `ui.close` its close mark sends. A click outside the pane also takes the keys, and the mod cannot tell it from Escape. In the Claude app, expanding or collapsing the pane takes the keys too. The engine raises no expand event, and the drawing without the keys comes about 60 to 90 ms before the one at the new width, so the mod cannot tell an expand from a click in the composer. | `hooks/mod/mod-pane-open.ts:openPane`, `hooks/mod/mod-pane-open.ts:matchPaneEscape`, `hooks/register.tsx:on('ui.render')` for `Pane`, `codex/src/tab.tsx` keydown handler |
