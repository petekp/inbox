# Owners: presses

Which file owns each rule about a press: what it reads on its way, where its status sits, which button's press is out, and the reducer that applies it. Row rules are in [Rows, presses and feedback](owners-rows-and-feedback.md).

| Rule | Owner | Read by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What a press reads at each step of its message's way: an answer as its option, with the step before it when it waits, as "Queued: Yes" and "Not confirmed: Yes"; a reply with the words the person typed in every step, as "Sending reply: …", "Queued reply: …", "Reply not confirmed: …" and "Replied: …", on one line. A reply saved before the inbox kept its words reads as before, as "Queued: Reply" and "Replied". | `hooks/rows.ts:deliveryLabel`, `hooks/rows.ts:replyLine`, `hooks/rows.ts:settledLabel` over `hooks/rows.ts:sendStepOf`, and `hooks/feedback.ts:doneLabel` for the resting label. `hooks/pressed.ts:pressed` keeps the words as `LastAction.reply`, and `hooks/view.ts:inboxView` sets `SettledState.reply` on a row a reply settled after its close. | All four looks, through `feedbackText` on an open or handed-off row and `settledLabel` on a settled row. Each look cuts the line to its width: the terminal with `truncate-end`, desktop with `clipLabel` at its characters per cell, Codex with `text-overflow: ellipsis`, and omp with `fit`. |
| Where a press status sits on an open row: under the buttons while its press is out, including an open or a copy, then after the title. A row that still waits after its press, such as a failing check after Fix, keeps its done status under the buttons. A Mark press, such as Dismiss, Done, an answer, or Set goal and Remove on the goal, draws no status under the buttons while it is out, because its reply changes the row, the PR block or the goal in one move: a status that grew in would shrink again with that change. Its button draws out instead, and a status the row drew before the press stays. The Codex tab still shows "Sending…" in an ask row's side column, which moves nothing, and the Herdr panel shows nothing more, as its differences say | `hooks/feedback.ts:isStatusUnderActions`, `hooks/feedback.ts:isPressOut`, `hooks/feedback.ts:isAfterTitle`, `hooks/feedback.ts:STILL_WAITING_AFTER`, `hooks/feedback.ts:holdsStatusWhileOut` | All four looks. Only the terminal and desktop looks read `isAfterTitle`, which puts a failure on its own line. Only Codex and Herdr read `holdsStatusWhileOut`: they wait on a reply while a press is out, and the terminal, desktop and omp looks apply a press in the draw that shows it. |
| Which button's press is out: it draws dim and ignores another press, by click or key, until the press settles. A note on its row, such as "This changed before your press", does not settle it. A press that reads Not confirmed and a reply are never out. | `hooks/feedback.ts:outAction` over `hooks/feedback.ts:pressFeedbackIn`, the row's feedback without its note, read into `ActionView.isOut` and `PrAction.isOut`; `hooks/session-presses.ts:pressOn` ignores the press by the same rule | All four looks draw it in their own way: see "Pressed button while its press is out" in Differences on purpose. |
| Press reducer and effects | `hooks/press-reducer.ts:applyPress`, `hooks/press-effects.ts:runLocalEffects`, `hooks/press-effects.ts:runSends` | The mod and omp through `hooks/host-press.ts:startPress`, and the Codex server |
| When a sent reply's draft is deleted | `hooks/press-reducer.ts:isPressConfirmed` | The terminal and desktop looks. A draft is deleted only after a confirmed press. A failed, stale or unconfirmed send keeps it, so Reply opens with the words. The mod's `runPress` calls it. The Codex tab, omp and Herdr keep the draft until the reply arrives, through `hooks/ui-controller-reply.ts:releasedDrafts`. While the send is still out, the looks differ: see "Reply while its send is out" in Differences on purpose. |
| When a sent reply's draft is deleted, once a look reads it from the view: when the row's last action is newer than at the press, its reply is the sent text, and its delivery is neither held, queued, failed nor unconfirmed. A last action that vanished deletes it only after a confirmed press. A draft edited after the send, or with its field open, stays. A press with no answer gives up after `PRESS_TIMEOUT_MS`, unless its row's message is held or queued. A refused or stale reply reopens its field when the same tab shows and the person gave no input after the press. If the controller moved the cursor meanwhile, as when the host saved the row as answered, the cursor goes back to the row. | `hooks/ui-controller-reply.ts:releasedDrafts`, `hooks/ui-controller-reply.ts:expirePresses`, `hooks/ui-controller-reply.ts:pressSettled` | omp and Herdr, through `omp/src/controller.ts:createPanel` and `herdr/src/main.ts:Inbox`. Until each other look moves onto the controller, the row above applies. |
| Press guard | `hooks/timing.ts:PRESS_GUARD_MS` | The terminal and desktop looks. omp guards only key presses on the ask View opens, through `hooks/ui-controller-selection.ts:isGuardedPress`. The Codex tab guards through the same rule: a click that selects a row guards that row from a second click, and View opening an ask by key guards that row's key presses. |
