# Owners: presses

Which file owns each rule about a press: what it reads on its way, where its status sits, which button's press is out, and the reducer that applies it. Row rules are in [Rows, presses and feedback](owners-rows-and-feedback.md).

| Rule | Owner | Read by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What a press reads at each step of its message's way: an answer as its option, with the step before it when it waits, as "Queued: Yes" and "Not confirmed: Yes"; a reply with the words the person typed in every step, as "Sending reply: …", "Queued reply: …", "Reply not confirmed: …" and "Replied: …", on one line. A reply saved before the inbox kept its words reads as before, as "Queued: Reply" and "Replied". | `hooks/rows.ts:deliveryLabel`, `hooks/rows.ts:replyLine`, `hooks/rows.ts:settledLabel` over `hooks/rows.ts:sendStepOf`, and `hooks/feedback.ts:doneLabel` for the resting label. `hooks/pressed.ts:pressed` keeps the words as `LastAction.reply`, and `hooks/view.ts:inboxView` sets `SettledState.reply` on a row a reply settled after its close. | All four looks, through `feedbackText` on an open or handed-off row and `settledLabel` on a settled row. Each look cuts the line to its width: the terminal with `truncate-end`, desktop with `clipLabel` at its characters per cell, Codex with `text-overflow: ellipsis`, and omp with `fit`. |
| Where a press status sits on an open row: under the buttons while its press is out, including an open or a copy, then after the title. A row that still waits after its press, such as a failing check after Fix, keeps its done status under the buttons. A Mark press, such as Dismiss, draws no status of its own while it is out, because its reply closes the row or PR block: a status that grew in would shrink again with the close. Its button draws out instead, and a status the row drew before the press stays | `hooks/feedback.ts:isStatusUnderActions`, `hooks/feedback.ts:isPressOut`, `hooks/feedback.ts:isAfterTitle`, `hooks/feedback.ts:STILL_WAITING_AFTER`, `hooks/row-motion.ts:holdsStatusWhileOut` | All four looks. Only the terminal and desktop looks read `isAfterTitle`, which puts a failure on its own line. Only Codex and Herdr read `holdsStatusWhileOut`: they wait on a reply while a press is out, and the terminal, desktop and omp looks apply a press in the draw that shows it. |
| Which button's press is out: it draws dim and ignores another press, by click or key, until the press settles. A note on its row, such as "This changed before your press", does not settle it. A press that reads Not confirmed and a reply are never out. | `hooks/feedback.ts:outAction` over `hooks/feedback.ts:pressFeedbackIn`, the row's feedback without its note, read into `ActionView.isOut` and `PrAction.isOut`; `hooks/session-presses.ts:pressOn` ignores the press by the same rule | All four looks draw it in their own way: see "Pressed button while its press is out" in Differences on purpose. |
| Press reducer and effects | `hooks/press-reducer.ts:applyPress`, `hooks/press-effects.ts:runLocalEffects`, `hooks/press-effects.ts:runSends` | The mod and omp through `hooks/host-press.ts:startPress`, and the Codex server |
| When a sent reply's draft is deleted | `hooks/press-reducer.ts:isPressConfirmed` | All five looks. A draft is deleted only after a confirmed press. A failed, stale or unconfirmed send keeps it, so Reply opens with the words. The mod's `runPress`, the Codex tab's `sendReply` and omp's `createPanel` each call it. The Herdr look calls it in `herdr/src/out.ts:isSentConfirmed`, and `herdr/src/keys.ts:withoutConfirmed` deletes the draft. While the send is still out, the looks differ: see "Reply while its send is out" in Differences on purpose. |
| Press guard | `hooks/timing.ts:PRESS_GUARD_MS` | The terminal, desktop and Codex looks. omp guards only the ask View opens. |
