# Differences on purpose: the Herdr look's motion after input

Part of [the look map](../looks.md#differences-on-purpose).

This part of the Herdr look's table covers how blocks move after the person's own key, click or press. [The Herdr look's motion](differences-herdr-motion.md) covers the rest. Like [the rest of the table](differences-herdr.md), it compares the Herdr look with the Codex tab.

| Topic | Herdr look | Why | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Motion after input | The person's own moves do not wait for a leave. A draw that brings the host's answer to their press counts as their input too, as when the host runs the press, the press's row changes, or an open or a copy the look runs itself ends, so what the answer brings shows at once. A block that a read changed in the same frame as their move is not theirs, and moves as a publish does (`FetchedReads`). In a draw that follows a key, click or press, a row the selection opens waits only for the closing panel's fade, about 120 to 136 ms. A press that brings a status under its buttons, as `o`'s "✓ Opened the log", opens it at once instead. Its panel shows at `FAINT_OPACITY` in that draw, holds faint until its box is nearly open, then fades to full, so the growing box cuts it only while it is that faint. A field the person's input brings opens its box in that draw. Its text field shows at `FAINT_OPACITY` at once and fades to full once the box is nearly open, so the growing box cuts it only while it is that faint. A field that comes without their input enters as any block does. Its buttons fade in once the box is nearly open, so the box's edge never cuts them. A row's Reply field does the same, its buttons waiting for the row's box to grow to the field's measured height. A row that closes while they wait keeps them hidden as its panel fades. The lines it takes the place of, such as the goal's text, hide at once and shut without holding anything. A field closed while its box is still short of open, by more than an entering block waits for before its content fades in, hides at once and its box springs shut from where it is, while the lines it took the place of grow back from where they are. A field closed once its box is nearly open leaves as any block does. So the page never passes its closed height. | Every press shows what it did in its first draw, since a person whose press showed nothing pressed it again. A growing box cuts a line, so what a press brings before its box is open shows at `FAINT_OPACITY`, under `CUT_OPACITY`, and goes full once the box is nearly open. A row the person selects while another row's panel fades waits for that fade, so the list does not grow before it shrinks. A press on that row has nothing to show until the row opens, so it opens at once. | `herdr/src/motion.ts:moveRows`, `herdr/src/panel-steps.ts:bringsStatusUnder`, `herdr/src/panel-steps.ts:panelPressOf`, `herdr/src/fetched.ts:FetchedReads`, `herdr/src/field-wait.ts:stepFieldWait` |
