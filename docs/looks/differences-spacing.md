# Differences on purpose: spacing

Part of [the look map](../looks.md#differences-on-purpose).

Every look takes its spacing from the scale in `hooks/layout.ts`. The scale is in points on a 4 pt unit. Its gaps rise in this order: `INSIDE_GAP_PT` (0), `LIST_GAP_PT` (4), `CHILD_GAP_PT` (8), `ITEM_GAP_PT` (12), `SECTION_GAP_PT` (24). A row's padding counts toward the gap beside it, so a closed row pads `ROW_PAD_PT`, half of `LIST_GAP_PT`, and an open row pads `OPEN_ROW_PAD_PT`, half of `ITEM_GAP_PT`. The rows below say how each look turns the scale into its own units, and where it cannot follow it. The Herdr look follows the scale in points, as its table says, and omp's row is in its own table.

| Topic | Terminal | Desktop | Codex | Why | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The unit a step draws in | Whole rows. `ITEM_GAP_PT` and `SECTION_GAP_PT` round to one blank row, and the smaller steps to none. Docked, a blank row goes before a top-level Status row when it or the row above takes more than one line. Inline, Status rows sit flush. The other tabs keep the blank rows and dividers they drew before the scale. | Lines of `DESKTOP_LINE_PT`. A step becomes its points over that line. A Box draws padding, margin and gap at half the written value, so a written value is twice the space it draws (`desktopSpaceLines`). A Status row pads half of `LIST_GAP_PT` when it shows one line and half of `ITEM_GAP_PT` when it shows more (`hooks/pane-sizes.ts:statusPadY`). | CSS pixels, which are points on macOS. `applyPageVariables` sets one CSS variable for each step, and the CSS reads only those. A Status row pads as desktop's does, and an open row takes `OPEN_ROW_PAD_PT`. | The terminal draws only whole rows, so a step under one row cannot show. The inline pane takes a third of the terminal by default, so a blank row between items there would cost a row of work each. The desktop pane counts space in lines and halves a Box's spacing. | `hooks/layout.ts`, `hooks/pane-status-tops.ts:spacedItems`, `hooks/pane-sizes.ts`, `codex/src/tab-motion.ts:applyPageVariables`, `codex/src/tab-css/` |
| Sizes the host sets | The terminal's cell is the unit. | The app sets the text size, the line and the height of a native button, so buttons and text do not sit on the 4 pt grid. Widths round to whole cells of `DESKTOP_CELL_PT`. A row frame's 1 pt border adds to the gap around it. | Tree lines, icons, borders and radii keep their own pixel sizes, since they size a stroke or a glyph, not a gap. | A look draws its host's own controls and type. | `hooks/pane-sizes.ts`, `hooks/pane-desktop-tree.tsx`, `codex/src/tab-css/rows.css` |
