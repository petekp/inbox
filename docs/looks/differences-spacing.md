# Differences on purpose: spacing

Part of [the look map](../looks.md#differences-on-purpose).

Every look takes its spacing from the scale in `hooks/layout.ts`. The scale is in points on a 4 pt unit. Its gaps rise in this order: `INSIDE_GAP_PT` (0), `LIST_GAP_PT` (4), `CHILD_GAP_PT` (8), `ITEM_GAP_PT` (12), `SECTION_GAP_PT` (24). A list's rows sit `LIST_MARGIN_PT` (4) clear of their group's title above and their Closed fold below, measured from the rows' boxes. A row's padding counts toward the gap beside it, so every row, closed or open, pads `ROW_PAD_PT`, half of `ITEM_GAP_PT` (6). Rows sit `ITEM_GAP_PT` apart with their boxes touching, so a closed one-line row is 32 pt tall to click and no space between two rows belongs to neither. The rows below say how each look turns the scale into its own units, and where it cannot follow it. The Herdr look follows the scale in points, as its table says, and omp's row is in its own table.
