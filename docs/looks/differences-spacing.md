# Differences on purpose: spacing

Part of [the look map](../looks.md#differences-on-purpose).

Every look takes its spacing from the scale in `hooks/layout.ts`. The scale is in points on a 4 pt unit. Its gaps rise in this order: `INSIDE_GAP_PT` (0), `LIST_GAP_PT` (4), `CHILD_GAP_PT` (8), `ITEM_GAP_PT` (12), `SECTION_GAP_PT` (24). A row's padding counts toward the gap beside it, so every row, closed or open, pads `ROW_PAD_PT`, half of `ITEM_GAP_PT` (6). Rows sit `ITEM_GAP_PT` apart with their boxes touching, so a closed one-line row is 32 pt tall to click and no space between two rows belongs to neither. The rows below say how each look turns the scale into its own units, and where it cannot follow it. The Herdr look follows the scale in points, as its table says, and omp's row is in its own table.

| Topic | Terminal | Desktop | Codex | Why | Where |

| The unit a step draws in | Whole rows. `ITEM_GAP_PT` and `SECTION_GAP_PT` round to one blank row, and the smaller steps to none, so a press's result sits on the line under its buttons with no blank row between. Docked, a blank row goes where `statusGaps` sets `ITEM_GAP_PT`: before a top-level Status row when it or the item above takes more than one line, counting a parent's nested rows in its item. Inline, Status rows sit flush. The other tabs keep the blank rows and dividers they drew before the scale. | Lines of `DESKTOP_LINE_PT`. A step becomes its points over that line. A Box draws padding, margin and gap at half the written value, so a written value is twice the space it draws (`desktopSpaceLines`). A Status row pads half the gap `statusGaps` sets above it and half the gap below it (`hooks/pane-sizes.ts:desktopPads`). | CSS pixels, which are points on macOS. `applyPageVariables` sets one CSS variable for each step, and the CSS reads only those. A Status row pads as desktop's does, from `statusRowPads`. | The terminal draws only whole rows, so a step under one row cannot show.
