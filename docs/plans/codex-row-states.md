# Codex row states

Status: built.

Every row in the Codex tab moves through one state machine, in `core/view/row-motion.ts`. The machine decides what each row draws and which animation runs. Before it, that decision was spread over separate sets in `codex/src/tab.tsx`, and each fix covered one transition.

## What it fixed

Measured in the harness on 2026-10-10:

- **Dismiss, Done and answers.** The open row turned into its settled line in one draw. Its body and buttons vanished in the frame the reply arrived, and the row dropped from 108 px to 44 px with no animation.
- **A settling row measured its end height while an earlier animation still held it.** When a press's reply came back while the row was still growing to show "Sending…", the collapse measured the held height. The row then sat at 80 px for 200 ms and jumped to 44 px.

The simpler fix was to extend the switch-rows code, `closingRows` and `collapse()`, to the settle case. One machine for every state replaced it, so a new transition cannot be added without a defined start, end and animation.

## The states

Each row the shown tab draws, keyed by its id, is in one state:

| State | Draws | Comes to rest as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `entering` | The row the view just brought in, growing from 0 height, then fading in over `ENTER_FADE_MS` once its space is nearly open | `closed` or `open`, the form it arrived in |
| `closed` | The line | |
| `opening` | Line and panel, the height growing to fit | `open` |
| `open` | Line and panel | |
| `closing` | The line, and the panel, inert, fading out over `LEAVE_FADE_MS` while the height holds, then the height shrinking | `closed` |
| `settling` | The settled line, under the form the row leaves while that form fades out, then while the height shrinks to it | `settled` |
| `settled` | The settled line, with Undo and its countdown when the person's own Done or Dismiss closed it | |
| `undoing` | The settled line while an Undo press is out: Undo's label and arrow dim, the arrow turns and the countdown pauses | |
| `leaving` | The row's last form, fading out over `LEAVE_FADE_MS` while its height holds, then collapsing to 0 | Removed. A settled row counts in the Closed fold from the start of its exit |

Every height moves on `HEIGHT_SPRING` in `core/view/springs.ts`, the spring the Herdr panel uses, which does not overshoot. A move from rest, or one turned back mid-way, comes to rest within `HEIGHT_SETTLE_MS`, 528 ms, so each moving state lasts that long, as `ROW_STATE_MS` in `core/view/row-motion.ts` says. Content that leaves fades out over `LEAVE_FADE_MS`, 120 ms, while its space holds its height, and its space closes as soon as the fade ends, so a leaving row stays `leaving`, and a closing row `closing`, for `LEAVE_FADE_MS` more. A closing row's panel fades out while the row holds its height, and then the height shrinks. A panel that shows nothing yet, as one still waiting to fade in, hides at once and holds nothing, and its Holds up line takes its closed form at once. Content that enters waits while its space opens, until the space stays within half of `SLIDE_PX` of open (`enterDelayMs`), which for a space that was shutting counts from its speed as well as its height, then fades in over `ENTER_FADE_MS`, 150 ms. So no text is cut by a moving space. While a block that leaves holds its space, nothing a read brings in grows into the list before that space starts to close: a row that enters, a row the read selects and the row it closes wait for it, and so does a group's end that grows to hold its empty note. A row that opens fades its panel in once its space is nearly open, the same for the person's own key, click or press as for a read. A row turned back from a close fades its panel back at once, from the opacity it shows. If its space had begun to shrink, the panel fades to 0.5 opacity until the space is nearly open, then to full over `ENTER_FADE_MS` (`turnBackFades`), each step once the one before it ends. So the space's edge cuts the panel's text only while it is under 0.5 opacity. Its height starts to move in the draw that opens it. While a closing row's panel fades, a row that opens or enters waits to grow, the person's own moves included, so the two heights start together and the list never grows before it shrinks. The opened row shows its selection at once. The person's own moves do not wait for a leave: in a draw that is not a read, the row the selection opens and the row it closes start to move together. A leave turned back fades back in from where it is. If its space had begun to shrink, or it left before its space opened, it waits while another leave holds its space, as a row that enters does. A glide already under way keeps moving, and a row's own change of height, such as a new status line, never waits. A row that a later draw restarts while it still waits keeps what is left of its wait, counted from when the wait started, so a redraw during the wait does not open the row early. A move that the person's own press or selection starts never waits for a leave. A settling row's fill fades over `FADE_MS`, 200 ms, since it is not content in a space that closes. A closing row's fill fades out over `LEAVE_FADE_MS` with its panel, and an opening row shows its fill at once. A row, line or fold that enters or leaves also slides with its fade, `SLIDE_PX` (8 px) above its place when faded out: it slides up as it leaves and down as it enters, and nothing scales. The offset follows the opacity, so a move turned around mid-way slides back from where it is. With reduced motion there is no slide. The tab samples the spring once a frame and plays the positions as keyframes through `el.animate`.

At each draw the tab works out the form each row should have now, its target:

| Target | When |
| --- | --- |
| `open` | The view lists the row open or held, and it is the tab's open row |
| `closed` | The view lists the row open or held, and another row is open |
| `settled` | The view lists the row settled, and its time to show in place has not run out |
| `undoing` | As `settled`, while its Undo press is out |
| `gone` | The view no longer lists the row, its settled time ran out, or the tab no longer holds it |

A held row is drawn as an open row, so it needs no state of its own. When its reply sends, it moves through `settling`. After Discard, it moves through `leaving`.

`stepRow` takes a row's track and its target and returns the next track. A track is the row's state, when it entered it, and whether its open form is part of its move (`hasOpenForm`). The row draws its panel while that holds, except while it settles, when the panel belongs to the form it leaves (`drawsPanel`). When the target changes, the row starts moving toward it from where it is drawn: `opening` toward open, `closing` toward closed, `settling` toward settled, `leaving` toward gone. This holds for a row still growing in, too. A leaving row that is listed again grows back in. When a moving state's time has passed, it comes to rest.

Rules that hold in every state:

- **A panel is never removed while it shows.** A track that draws the panel keeps drawing it through `closing` and `leaving`, so it fades out while the row holds its height instead of vanishing. A settling row keeps its open form, panel included, drawn over its settled line while that form fades out.
- **A row that settles or comes back through Undo fades every line that changes.** Through its move, the row draws the form it leaves over the form it takes (`drawsFormLeft`). That form fades out over `LEAVE_FADE_MS` while the row's height holds, its panel from the opacity it showed. The titles cross-fade (`titleFades`), so the words they share never dim. Then the height moves to the new form's, and the new form's other lines fade in over `ENTER_FADE_MS`, each once its space is nearly open. The form it leaves keeps the press its line last drew, so "Sending…" fades with it. A row Undo opens shows its Holds up line only with its panel. The move lasts `LEAVE_FADE_MS` longer (`moveMs`). A row Undo brought back that opens or closes in the fade-out keeps the fade and moves after it. Another change turned back draws its new form at once, and a later one starts a new fade.
- **Nothing under the line moves while a press is out.** "Sending…" shows in the line's age slot. That slot is at least as wide as "Sending…" on every row, so the title keeps its width and does not wrap again when a press starts. The open row's panel runs under the side column, so the reserved width narrows only the title. A local press's "Opening x…" shows after the title, where its result will show.
- **A Mark press out grows nothing and drops nothing.** A PR block's Dismiss, or Clear merged and closed, draws its button out and no "Sending…" under the block's buttons, as `holdsStatusWhileOut` in `core/view/feedback.ts` says. The reply changes the block in one move, so a line that grew in for the press would shrink again, or turn back mid-growth when the reply landed after the first frame. A row's Dismiss, Done or answer keeps the status the row drew before the press, such as "✓ Explained" on its own line under the title, and only the side column reads "Sending…". Dropping that status in the press's draw would move the row's body and buttons up before the settle's fade starts.
- **A row that arrives fades in as a whole.**
- **Time ends a move, not the animation.** After each draw, the tab schedules one more draw for when the first moving row's move is due to end. That draw brings the row to rest, so an animation that never reports its end cannot hold a row.
- **A move starts from the drawn height, speed and opacity.** A row turned around mid-move, or closed while it still grows in, starts the new animation from where it is drawn, with the speed its height has there.
- **A resting row glides to a new height.** When the content of a `closed`, `open`, `settled` or `undoing` row changes height, the row's height moves to the new one on the height spring. Examples are the reply field opening and a title that wraps again. The reply field takes focus and shows its caret from the first frame of the glide. A row inside another moving element glides on its own, and the element around it follows at its natural height. A resting element whose content changes width, such as a tab whose count widens, glides its width the same way.
- **Lines enter and leave.** `stepLines` in `codex/src/tab-line-steps.ts` steps a list of lines through `stepRow`, the same as `stepLists` does for rows. It steps the Status page's lines, the goal's milestones and the lines under the goal's text, in `codex/src/tab-status-steps.ts`. It also steps the line a met goal's milestones fold into, the head drawn in its own section, and the Later label above the first milestone that starts later. A milestone's space above it grows from none as the milestone enters, and shrinks to none once its fade ends as it leaves. The milestones that fold opens to are the goal's milestones, so they grow in and leave as stepped lines, and stay at rest while the goal turns met or unmet with the fold open. It also steps the banners under the tab bar, the status an open row draws under its buttons while its press is out, and the block above an open row's buttons that holds its fold's note, its press note and its press error. A line the tab brings in grows in, and a line it drops leaves from its place, so its text stays until its space has closed. A line that grows in or leaves scales its vertical padding and top margin with its height, so it starts and ends at no height. A line with `data-rest` starts and ends at that many pixels instead. The block above a row's buttons sets it to the space the buttons gain once the block is gone, so the buttons end where they rest without it. The goal's heading glides when its height changes.
- **A settling PR block's body keeps its place.** When a PR block settles, the body it drew open stays at the top it had, so its buttons do not jump up while the height shrinks over them.
- **A row's end height is its content's height.** The tab cancels the row's own glide and measures the row before the next paint, so no animation of the row changes it. A group's empty line and fold take their end height the same way, so a draw in the middle of their move does not cut it short.
- **A leaving row keeps its place.** The tab draws it until it is removed, in its Asks group or its kind's Finds group, just after the last row above it last time that is still drawn (`withLeaving` in `core/view/row-motion.ts`). So it never splits a row that stays from the rows that sat under it last time. Lines, PR rows and PR blocks place leaving items the same way, and so does the Herdr panel.
- **A group's empty line and Closed fold take a leaving row's place.** A leaving row no longer counts in its group, so they show from the draw that starts its exit. One element holds both, with its content at its bottom. Its height grows on the height spring while the row collapses, so a fold already shown stays in view and the empty line slides in above it. The rows below move once, in one direction.
- **A row that arrives in an empty group takes the empty line's place.** While every row that stays in the group is entering, the empty line stays drawn, out of the flow, just above the fold. It fades out where it was, and the space it held closes on the height spring while the row grows over it. The rows below move only by the difference in height, in one direction. With reduced motion, rows do not enter, so the line goes in one draw.
- **A PR row moves as an item row does.** `stepPrs` in `codex/src/tab-pr-steps.ts` steps each PR row on the PRs tab through `stepRow`, the same as `stepLists` does for the other tabs. A row the view no longer lists, such as a cancelled check after Re-run, leaves from its place in its group.
- **A PR group leaves with its last row.** A group's title and rows share one element with its own track. Its title counts only the rows the view lists. When the view lists none, the group leaves on the height spring while its rows collapse inside it, so the title stays drawn until its last row has left.
- **A PR block moves as a row does.** `stepPrs` steps each block through `stepRow` on its own track: `open` while the view lists it open, `settled` or `undoing` once it is dismissed, and `gone` once the view no longer lists it. Dismiss, Clear merged and closed and a ranked PR's Dismiss all go through it, so each block settles on its own. A settling block draws its settled line over the body it last drew open, inert, in the place it had, while its height shrinks to the settled line's. The body stays the same element, so a row still closing, opening or leaving in it finishes its move. That block fades over 200 ms, since the settled block's padding would otherwise still show its top. Undo opens the block again, its height growing from the settled line's. A block the view no longer lists leaves from its place in its last form.
- **A block's padding is inside the element that animates,** so the block's height is its content's height and its end height measures right.
- **The PRs tab's own buttons leave with the last merged or closed PR.** "Clear merged and closed" has its own track. It leaves when the view no longer offers it, drawn inert, and grows in when Undo brings a merged or closed PR back.
- **A PR block's press line grows in.** The line under a block's buttons, such as "✓ Started a re-run of CI", has its own track. It enters when it first shows on a block the last draw drew, and leaves with its last text.
- **A closing row comes to rest at its closed line's height.** A closing row also draws its closed line's after-label and side column, hidden, so the collapse measures the height the row has once the move ends. A PR row's ran line, which only the closed form shows, is hidden while the row closes.
- **Rows of a tab not shown are forgotten.** They show their form at once when their tab shows.
- **Reduced motion.** Every move ends in the draw that starts it, including a resting row's glide, a Status line, a banner and a tab's width.
- **A row's title fades between its forms only while its line rests: to its closed form as a close starts, and to its open form once its line has grown.** While a row opens or closes, its line draws the title twice: in full, in flow, and clamped over the full title to as many lines as the closed row's title takes. Both forms draw the closed row's regular weight, which an open title keeps too, so they wrap alike and the words they share overlap exactly. Where the forms differ, the fade (`titleFades`) brings the form the title takes in over the whole fade and takes the form it leaves out over the second half. As a row closes, the title fades to its clamped form over `LEAVE_FADE_MS` while the line holds its height, as the row's panel fades out. The line then shrinks with its row. A line that holds for less than the fade takes the clamped form at once. As a row opens, the line grows from the clamped title's height with the clamped title shown. The title fades to its full form over `ENTER_FADE_MS` once the line comes to rest (`titleSwapMs`). So the line never cuts a line of the title that shows. Each form fades from the opacity it shows, so a fade that turns back never blinks. An open row keeps its clamped title drawn at no opacity, since the fade can end after `opening` does. The trunk the mark draws beside the title fades with the row's panel, so the tree comes and goes whole. A row's Holds up line fades between its two forms with the same timing: its closed form, one line under the title, and its open form, the first branch of the tree. The row draws its closed form in the same place while it draws its panel. As a row closes, the closed form fades in over `LEAVE_FADE_MS`, and the open form and the trunk it hangs from fade out over the second half. As a row opens, the closed form shows at once and fades out over the second half of the panel's fade-in, while the open form fades in with the panel. So the line never vanishes or gets cut off in one frame. A title the clamp shows whole keeps its full form through `closing`. A title keeps one weight in every state, since a weight change reflows its words. A move that turns back starts from where the line is. A measure of the row's natural height ignores a running line glide.
- **A connector fades with the rows it joins.** A tree line shows only while both things it joins show. A column between rows that stay never fades. A line that ends at a row that enters or leaves fades with that row: out while the row's space holds, and in on the row's own fade, once its space is nearly open (`showConnectors` in `codex/src/tab-connectors.ts`). A fade does not clip its row, so the strokes a row draws left of its panel fade with it. So no line shows while what it joins is hidden, and none fades while the rows it joins slide.
- **The line never moves** between `closed`, `opening`, `open` and `closing`, as [motion-and-undo.md](motion-and-undo.md) says.

## Not changing

- The Claude app's pane. Its engine has no opacity or CSS, and fast redraws break its clicks, so Dismiss there still swaps the row in one draw.
- The settle clock in `codex/src/settle.ts`, which the server shares. Changing it would change the server's build fingerprint.
