# Evals the release can trust: Status judge follow-ups

Part of [Evals the release can trust](eval-trust.md), split from [eval-trust-follow-ups.md](eval-trust-follow-ups.md). These are gaps in the Status judge, its instructions in `evals/status-judge/`, and the hand-written pages `./scripts/eval.sh --suite status --judge-pages` checks it on.

Status: building. Each item stays until the change that does it lands.

Each item names what it would catch. Remove an item when the change that does it lands.

- **The judge-pages check after #148.** The Status page's head is now a title with a description under it. The hand-written pages that `--judge-pages` checks the judge on save their states with a `goal`, which loads as a title with no description, so the judge never sees a head drawn with both lines. `evals/status-judge/context.md` also still says the head is "one goal line" today. Rewriting the pages' states with head descriptions would let the check catch a judge that passes a description naming finished work, or fails a good head for drawing two lines.
- **The judge made three mistakes on the hand-written pages in the 0.5.0 release pass.** The results are `status-judge-2026-11-16T23-52-21.996Z.json` for the top folder and `status-judge-2026-11-16T23-53-28.735Z.json` for `goal/`. Sharper wording for each check in `evals/status-judge/`, run on these three pages, would catch the judge passing a broken page or blaming the wrong check.
  - `s1-bad-each-once`: the judge passed `each-once` on a page that breaks it. A judge that passes bad pages makes judged Status checks pass more often than they should.
  - `s2-bad-rows-are-work`: the judge failed `head` on a page that breaks only `rows-are-work`. 0.4.0's pass, at 3701e97c, made the same mistake.
  - `s2-bad-goal-direction` in `goal/`: the judge failed `milestones` on a page that breaks only `goal`. A judged `milestones` failure in a replay could then be a goal problem.
  - Graded again with the `na` lists the 24 older pages gained, the answers from 0.4.0's pass at 3701e97c called 19 of 24 and 20 of 21 pages right (`status-judge-2026-11-15T08-13-38.693Z.json` and `status-judge-2026-11-15T08-14-25.406Z.json`). The judge failed `head` on `s1-good-3`, `s1-bad-was-lines`, `s2-bad-rows-are-work` and `s2-bad-child-labels`, passed `each-once` on `s1-bad-each-once`, and passed `was-lines` on `s2-bad-split-done-in-other`, which has no Prev line.
- **The judge called 41 of the 45 pages in `evals/judge-pages/` right in the pass at 889ce236** (`status-judge-2026-11-23T04-15-13.726Z.json`). Two misses repeat ones listed above: it passed `each-once` on `s1-bad-each-once`, and passed `was-lines` on `s2-bad-split-done-in-other`. Two are on pages no earlier pass missed. Sharper wording for `milestones` and `head` in `evals/status-judge/`, run on these two pages, would catch a judged failure that blames the wrong check.
  - `s1-bad-goal-agent-words`: the judge failed `milestones` on a page that breaks only `goal`, as with `s2-bad-goal-direction` above.
  - `s1-bad-milestones-task`: the judge failed `head` on a page that breaks only `milestones`.
- **Two pages the judge calls right only some of the time.** In five runs each on the judge text before and after the change to its goal-block sentence (11-24), `s2-bad-goal-not-the-person` was called right once in five on both, and `s1-bad-where-it-stands` four times in five on both. On `s2-bad-goal-not-the-person` the judge fails `milestones` on a page that breaks only `goal`, as on `s1-bad-goal-agent-words`, because the milestones belong to a goal the facts say is not set. On `s1-bad-where-it-stands` it sometimes fails `head`, calling the title "a sentence". So one `--judge-pages` run can show a page that was right going wrong with no change to the judge's text. Telling `milestones` to judge milestones against the goal the page shows, and rerunning these two pages five times, would catch a judged `milestones` failure that is really a `goal` problem.
- **`s2-bad-goal-direction` misses more often on the new text of item 8, and the page cannot test the goal rule.** The page breaks only `goal`. In 30 runs each (11-24), the judge wrongly failed `milestones` on it 8 times with the old text of item 8 in `evals/status-judge/context.md` and 22 times with the new text. The page never shows the line that item 8 describes. The judge's reasons cite the facts' `Goal: none set` line, because the facts give the person's words only inside the focus line. In a variant, the person's words moved from the focus line to a line `The person stated a goal at this step: "…"`. The judge then passed `goal` in 10 of 10 runs on both texts, which is a wrong call. The goal "Payroll that never needs manual fixes" repeats the person's words, and the rule against a direction with no finish line does not catch it. A page where the person states a direction, and the facts give a finish line that the page's goal leaves out, would catch a judge that passes a goal with no finish line.
- **`eval.sh --judge-pages` has no per-page rerun.** It calls the judge once on every page in a folder. A release pass that sees one wrong call cannot rerun that page alone, so it cannot tell a mistake the judge makes every time from a one-off. In the 0.5.0 pass nothing was rerun for that reason. A `--case <page>` option that takes `--runs` would let the pass rerun only the missed page, and catch a judge mistake that repeats.
- **The judge pages are drawn text, and their builders are outside Git.** `evals/judge-pages/` holds each page as the text the judge reads. The scripts that drew them from saved Status states, `build-pages.ts` and `goal/build-goal-pages.ts` in the main checkout's `.scratch/status-judge-pages/`, import `hooks/` paths that #704 moved, so they no longer run. After a change to how the page is drawn, the pages still show the old drawing. Moving the builders into `evals/`, split under the size limit, and checking in `check-cases` that they draw the pages in the folder would catch pages that no longer match the drawing.
