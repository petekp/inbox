# Codex row states on the Status page

Status: built.

These rules extend [codex-row-states.md](codex-row-states.md) to the Status page's lines.

- **Lines enter and leave.** `stepLines` in `codex/src/tab-line-steps.ts` steps a list of lines through `stepRow`, the same as `stepLists` does for rows. It steps the Status page's lines, the goal's milestones and the lines under the goal's text, in `codex/src/tab-status-steps.ts`. It also steps the line a met goal's milestones fold into, the head drawn in its own section, and the Later label above the first milestone that starts later. A milestone's space above it grows from none as the milestone enters, and shrinks to none once its fade ends as it leaves. When every milestone leaves, the list's space above them shrinks the same way, and the goal section's space below grows to its size without them as the last milestone's padding goes. A heading that starts its list counts only the rows that are not leaving above it, so it glides to its place as they close. So the rows below never jump when the leaving lines go. The milestones that fold opens to are the goal's milestones, so they grow in and leave as stepped lines, and stay at rest while the goal turns met or unmet with the fold open. It also steps the banners under the tab bar, the status an open row draws under its buttons while its press is out, and the block above an open row's buttons that holds its fold's note, its press note and its press error. A line the tab brings in grows in, and a line it drops leaves from its place, so its text stays until its space has closed. A line that grows in or leaves scales its vertical padding and top margin with its height, so it starts and ends at no height. A line with `data-rest` starts and ends at that many pixels instead. The block above a row's buttons sets it to the space the buttons gain once the block is gone, so the buttons end where they rest without it. The goal's heading glides when its height changes.
- **A subject that changes lists moves as one line.** A subject, and the rows under it, that Remove or Undo on the goal moves between the goal's milestones and the list after the goal is two rows with different keys: the old one leaves, as `leaving`, and the new one enters, as `entering` (`otherListKey` in `codex/src/tab-motion-keys.ts` names the other row). `moveLines` in `codex/src/tab-line-move.ts` draws a copy of the entering row over the page, starting at the leaving row's box. It moves to the entering row's own box, as it is at each frame, and its depth moves with it, so its mark, text and tree lines travel together. Its share of the way, `lineMoveShare` in `core/view/row-motion.ts`, follows the height spring, which does not overshoot, and lasts `LINE_MOVE_MS`. Both rows stay hidden while it moves, and the entering row shows when it ends. A row that enters with no row leaving from the other list is not moved. With reduced motion rows do not enter, so nothing moves. Herdr moves the same line as one block (`herdr/src/reshape-hold.ts`).
